Voice alternates: 8 October 2026

What this is. Three things, none of them in the game unless marked. Q01–Q12: the twelve Jack and Vera lines that were re-recorded at speaking volume on 8 October, with the sample that was not used and the hushed take they replaced. M, J, V, W: three alternate readings each of the peak lines (Mara in scene 71, Jack and Vera in scene 98, Vera in scene 74); the green row is what plays now. Voices: three new design previews for each voice that was picked blind, next to the voice in use. Pick by ear.

Level is the average loudness of the speech in dBFS (higher is louder; measured as the mean frame level within 30 dB of the peak). Jack and Vera speak at about −18 to −25 in the game; [weakly], [quietly] and [softly] read about 10 dB under that, so they get a red meter. Mara’s four [weakly] lines in scene 71 are left in the game on purpose: weak may be what the scene wants. [angrily] runs about 5 dB hotter than the rest.

To use one: copy its command and run it in the repo (needs FFMPEG), then npm run build:neonoire and re-import into the two games (voice README, “Reaching the games”). The take it replaces is archived, never lost. A voice preview is not a take: tell Claude which one you want and the line will be re-recorded in it.
