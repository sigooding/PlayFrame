Voices: three new design previews for each voice that was picked blind. Pick by ear.

Level is the average loudness of the speech in dBFS (higher is louder; measured as the mean frame level within 30 dB of the peak). Jack and Vera speak at about −18 to −25 in the game; [weakly], [quietly] and [softly] read about 10 dB under that, so they get a red meter. Mara’s four [weakly] lines in scene 71 are left in the game on purpose: weak may be what the scene wants. [angrily] runs about 5 dB hotter than the rest, and the descriptive directions sometimes run much longer than the line (a held laugh or sob), which the duration shows.

To use one: copy its command and run it in the repo (needs FFMPEG), then npm run build:neonoire and re-import into the two games (voice README, “Reaching the games”). The take it replaces is archived, never lost. A voice preview is not a take: tell Claude which one you want and the line will be re-recorded in it.
