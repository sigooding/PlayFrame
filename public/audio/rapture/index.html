Let the Raptures Commence: voices

Episode one, the draft of 21 September 2026.

Nobody has listened to any of this. Every voice was designed from the show bible's cast text and picked blind, by measured pitch, range, brightness and pace. The cast section lists every preview of every voice so you can pick by ear; the table read plays the recorded lines in screenplay order with the pauses the script spells out (the rest are estimates). Jodie is a light young adult voice: the design tool refuses a child's.

Table read

Press Play scene, or any line. Lines play at their place on the scene's timeline; the written pauses ("Four seconds of nothing", "An eight-second pause") are locked, the others are guesses for the action between lines.
