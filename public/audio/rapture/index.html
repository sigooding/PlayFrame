Let the Raptures Commence: voices

Episode one (the draft of 21 September 2026), then episodes two to five (the twelve shot boards, which carry their own dialogue).

Nobody has listened to any of this. Every voice was designed from the show bible's cast text and picked blind, by measured pitch, range, brightness and pace. The cast section lists every preview of every voice so you can pick by ear; the table read plays the recorded lines in screenplay order with the pauses the script spells out (the rest are estimates). Jodie is a light young adult voice: the design tool refuses a child's. Episodes two to five add 14 characters; the workspace's 30 custom-voice slots were full, so they speak in ready-made ElevenLabs voices used as they are (picked blind from measured previews; the 16 designs made for them are auditions in voices/eps2-5/, never saved).

Table read

Press Play scene, or any line. Lines play at their place on the scene's timeline; the written pauses ("Four seconds of nothing", "An eight-second pause") are locked, the others are guesses for the action between lines.
