## CHARACTERS (locked descriptions — repeated verbatim in every generation prompt)

### THE VICTIM — woman, mid-70s
Short neatly set white hair, thin face with deep gentle wrinkles, thin wire-rimmed glasses. **Wardrobe:** good dove-grey wool coat with a neat collar, muted floral silk scarf knotted at the throat, dark woollen skirt, thick tights, sensible black lace-up shoes. A worn brown leather clasp handbag hung over her left forearm. Small, upright, unhurried — the bearing of a woman who has walked this street for fifty years. Not panicking. Almost disappointed in him.

### THE MUGGER — male, 19
Skinny, wiry, hood of a black track top pulled up over his head, pale face with acne scarring on the cheeks, dark circles under the eyes, chapped lips, a thin gold earring in the left ear. Black tracksuit bottoms with a thin white piping line down the side seam, worn trainers. Carries a small cheap folding knife, short blade, held low and tight against his own body — never brandished, never raised. Under-rehearsed. More frightened than she is.

## CONTINUITY NOTES

- **The handbag** (worn brown leather, brass clasp, short strap over the left forearm) is her spine: over the forearm (1, 2) → unclipped calmly (7) → held out, other hand steadying the clasp (8) → still offered to empty air (11, 12) → clipped shut (15) → receives the knife wrapped in a tissue (17).
- **The knife** is his spine: low and tight (3, 4, 7) → shaking (6) → on the pavement, ringing (10) → her shoe beside it (16) → lifted in a tissue, into her bag (17). The street keeps nothing of him.
- **The doorway** where he came from stays in frame in 12 and 18 — dark and empty. The only evidence the audience ever gets.
- **The camera is a character.** After shot 9 it behaves like an operator who has lost his subject: drifting, hunting, slightly late to the reframe. Human, not effect.
- **Her temperature:** she never panics. Disappointed (5), practical (7, 8), still (11, 12), embarrassed at looking up (13), concerned *for him* (14), orderly (15, 17). Play every beat at her pace.
- **Sound design (for the edit):** flat street tone, muffled traffic, no score. The knife's ring on the pavement at 10 is the only real sound.
