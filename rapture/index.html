Cold Open Act One · Scene 1

The Look

Format2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen
GradeSodium amber key, halation bloom; inky blue-grey shadows
TexturePainterly nocturne, fine grain, mirror-wet asphalt
ScoreNone. The knife's ring at shot 10 is the only real sound.
The EventNo flash, no glow, no wind. The camera just loses him.
CameraHandheld where marked; static frames CCTV-still. After 9 it hunts.

Cast

THE VICTIM — woman, mid-70s. Short neatly set white hair, wire-rimmed glasses, good dove-grey wool coat, muted floral silk scarf, dark woollen skirt, sensible black lace-up shoes, worn brown leather clasp handbag over her left forearm. She never panics. Almost disappointed in him.

THE MUGGER — male, 19. Skinny, hood of a black track top up, pale face with acne scarring, dark circles, thin gold earring, black tracksuit bottoms with a white piping line. Knife held low and tight to his own body — never raised. More frightened than she is.

Continuity spines — the handbag: forearm → unclipped → held out → still offered → clipped shut → receives the knife in a tissue. The knife: low and tight → shaking → ringing on tarmac → her shoe beside it → lifted in a tissue, into her bag. The street keeps nothing of him.
