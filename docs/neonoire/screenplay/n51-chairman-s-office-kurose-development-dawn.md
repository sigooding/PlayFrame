Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Kurose, Detective Ishida.
Grammar: 35mm static; two men and a model of tomorrow.

INT. CHAIRMAN'S OFFICE, KUROSE DEVELOPMENT - DAWN #51#

The glass walls grey with dawn. Kurose sits in a leather chair beside the model of the redevelopment, a cup of tea on the arm of the chair.

Standing before him, hat in hand, raincoat still wet: Detective ISHIDA.

It is the first time we see them together. It explains everything.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
I told you. Leave him alone. Let him find it.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
You told me he was tired, Ishida. That he had given up twenty years ago. He doesn't look tired to me.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
He trusts me. He'll bring it to me. He said so.
(beat)
Then we take both. The girl and the thing.

Kurose sips his tea. He looks down at the model: the little plaza with its fountain where the Hive still stands.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
You were always very careful. I hope you still are.

Ishida bows. Holds the bow a moment too long.
