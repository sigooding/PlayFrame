Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading. Written, not boarded: the numbered shot board covers the opening scenes only.
Cast: — (not boarded; the draft names its own cast).
Grammar: Tokyo as a memory that is still happening. The city lights the characters, not the sky: vending machines, shop signs, train windows, fluorescent 
tubes. Sodium orange against a sick fluorescent green. Soft halation around every light, blacks slightly crushed. It should look like film, and feel 
like something remembered. Rain is never glamorous — no lightning, no storms, cold, steady, patient rain that turns the streets black and reflective. 
Wide and patient; close-ups are rare, so they count. Nothing is explained.

INT. CHAIRMAN'S OFFICE, KUROSE DEVELOPMENT - DAWN #51#

The glass walls grey with dawn. Kurose sits in a leather chair beside the model of the redevelopment, a cup of tea on the arm of the chair.

Standing before him, hat in hand, raincoat still wet: Detective ISHIDA.

It is the first time we see them together. It explains everything.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
I told you. Leave him alone. Let him find it.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
You told me he was tired, Ishida. That he had given up twenty years ago. He doesn't look tired to me.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
He trusts me. He'll bring it to me. He said so.
(beat)
Then we take both. The girl and the thing.

Kurose sips his tea. He looks down at the model: the little plaza with its fountain where the Hive still stands.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
You were always very careful. I hope you still are.

Ishida bows. Holds the bow a moment too long.
