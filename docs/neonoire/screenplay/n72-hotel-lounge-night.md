INT. HOTEL LOUNGE - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Vera Voss.
Grammar: Tokyo Story in colour: low, level, static 50mm. Vera pretty, dry and intact until rain. No push-in; piano plays; umbrella stays.

INT. HOTEL LOUNGE - NIGHT #72#

The clock above the bar: 11:52.

Vera's gin and tonic sits untouched. The ice has melted. The lime has sunk.

The pianist plays something slow. The same old ballad from the neighbour's radio, only now on a piano, stripped of its words.

Vera looks at the door. At her phone. No messages.

She takes out the lighter and turns it over in her fingers. Open. Shut.

BARTENDER
(in Japanese)
Another?

VERA
(in Japanese)
No. Thank you.

She almost laughs at herself. She looks down at the red dress. She feels foolish, and then hurt, and then angry.

Her phone RINGS.

She grabs it. The screen: ISHIDA.

Her face changes. She answers.

VERA (CONT'D)
Hello?

ISHIDA (V.O.)
(filtered; gently)
Miss Voss. I'm so sorry to call so late.

She knows. From his voice, she already knows. She stands up.

ISHIDA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
Your sister was found tonight. I'm very sorry. She died a short time ago.

Vera doesn't move. The piano plays on.

ISHIDA (V.O.) (CONT'D)
She was with Mr. Jack. He had been hiding her for several days, it seems. In the Hive.
(beat)
I'm sorry. I thought you knew.

The phone slowly lowers from her ear.

The pianist plays. The clock ticks. Somewhere a glass is set down.

Vera walks out of the lounge. Not running yet. Walking very carefully, as if the floor might give way.

The blue umbrella stays where she left it, leaning against the empty stool.
