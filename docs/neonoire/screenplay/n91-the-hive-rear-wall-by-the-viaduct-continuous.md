EXT. THE HIVE, REAR WALL BY THE VIADUCT - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Vera Voss.
Grammar: 24mm on the iron ladder against the viaduct wall; 50mm on her hand in his as the train covers the descent.

EXT. THE HIVE, REAR WALL BY THE VIADUCT - CONTINUOUS #91#

Rain. The rear wall of the low wing, pressed against the concrete viaduct. Vera comes out of the window onto the ladder. Jack after her, pulling the shutter to behind him.

Above, a flashlight beam sweeps the window and finds only a closed shutter.

Overhead the rails begin to sing. A light grows at the far end of the viaduct.

A train. It arrives, a roaring wall of noise and wind and lit windows, and under it the two of them go down the ladder, rung by rung, and nobody hears a thing. Passengers inside reading, sleeping, looking at their phones. None of them see.

Her hand is in his on the rail. At the bottom she doesn't take it back.

When the last carriage has passed, the ladder is empty.
