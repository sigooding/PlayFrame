INT. SMALL BAR, KANDA - DAY #81#

The bar in daylight. Chairs on the tables. Okada behind the counter.

He sees Jack's face and his hands, and he doesn't ask. He opens the cash drawer, lifts out the tray, and hands Jack the cassette.

Jack holds it.

OKADA
The girl?

Jack can't answer.

Okada closes his eyes. Then he pours two small glasses and puts one in front of Jack. They drink together, without a toast.
