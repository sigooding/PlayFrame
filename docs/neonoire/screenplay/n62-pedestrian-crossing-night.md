EXT. PEDESTRIAN CROSSING - NIGHT #62#

The same small intersection as before. The signal is red. Vera and Jack stand at the kerb under his cheap plastic umbrella.

JACK
Tomorrow night. Will you have dinner with me?

She looks at him, surprised.

VERA
Like a date?

JACK
Like a date.

VERA
Jack, my sister --

JACK
I know.
(beat)
There's something I need to tell you. I want to tell you properly. Not in an office. Not on a train.

She searches his face. Whatever she finds there makes her smile. Properly. For the first time in the film.

VERA
Okay.

JACK
The old hotel by the station. The lounge. Ten o'clock.

VERA
Ten o'clock.

The signal turns green. The old melody begins to CHIME.

They cross. She takes his arm.
