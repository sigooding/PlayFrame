EXT. POLICE STATION - NIGHT #93#

Rain. Ishida comes down the front steps without an umbrella.

At the kerb, a black sedan. Engine running. Wipers beating slowly.

He stops. He knows this car.

A long moment. Then the rear door opens from inside.
