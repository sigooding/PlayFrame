INT. ROADSIDE INN, KITCHEN DOORWAY - NIGHT #37#

Jack can't sleep. He wanders back down the corridor.

The kitchen's back door stands open to the rain. Mrs. Noda sits on the step outside under the eave, smoking.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Don't tell my husband.

JACK
(in Japanese)
I quit.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Then you'll understand why I don't want him to know.

She offers the pack anyway. He shakes his head. He stands in the doorway and looks out: a muddy yard, a gate onto a farm track, and the old pickup parked crooked across the path.

MRS. NODA (CONT'D)
(in Japanese)
Sorry about the truck. He parks it like he's drunk. He's never drunk. The keys are always in it. Who would steal that?

Jack looks at the truck. He says nothing.
