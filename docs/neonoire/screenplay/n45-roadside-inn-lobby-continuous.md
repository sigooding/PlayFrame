INT. ROADSIDE INN, LOBBY - CONTINUOUS #45#

From the corridor, Jack can see the lobby. One masked man stands in the middle of the room, weapon up, covering the stairs. The other has gone up.

Mr. Noda is behind the counter, out of the gunman's line of sight. He sees Jack. Jack puts a finger to his lips.

Jack reaches out and flips the lobby light switch. DARK.

Only the TV lights the room now: blue, flickering.

The masked man spins and fires toward the corridor. The shots stitch across the wall above Jack's head. One tears through the beer vending machine by the door.

It lights up and CHIMES:

VENDING MACHINE
(recorded; in Japanese)
Thank you very much.

The gunman flinches toward the sound.

In that half-second, Jack is across the dark lobby, behind the counter. He grabs Mr. Noda by the collar and drags him down the corridor.

On the wall, the pink payphone starts to RING.

No one answers it. It rings and rings.
