Grammar: Tokyo as a memory that is still happening. The city lights the characters, not the sky: vending machines, shop signs, train windows, fluorescent 
tubes. Sodium orange against a sick fluorescent green. Soft halation around every light, blacks slightly crushed. It should look like film, and feel 
like something remembered. Rain is never glamorous — no lightning, no storms, cold, steady, patient rain that turns the streets black and reflective. 
Wide and patient; close-ups are rare, so they count. Nothing is explained.

INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - CONTINUOUS #20#

Flour sacks. The futon. The bare bulb.

Mara stands against the far wall with a kitchen knife held in both hands. Not well. Her hands are shaking.

Jack stops in the curtain. He holds his hands open where she can see them.

JACK
My name is Jack. Your sister hired me.

MARA
Prove it.

He takes the red bird clip from his pocket. Holds it out on his open palm.

Mara stares at it. The knife lowers an inch.

JACK
She says it was hers. She says you stole it when you were kids and never gave it back.

Something in Mara's face gives way. The knife drops to her side.

MARA
It was never hers. Dad gave it to me. She just says that.

She almost laughs, and then she is crying, silently, pressing the back of her wrist against her mouth.

Jack waits. He doesn't go to her. He sits down on a flour sack, slowly, making himself smaller.

JACK
Is she okay? Is she -- does she know you're --

MARA
Is she okay?

JACK
She's been calling you. She hasn't slept.

MARA
I know. I listen to them.
(beat)
Don't tell her I'm here.

JACK
She's your sister.

MARA
They have my purse. They know my name. They know where I live, where she lives, all of it. If she knows where I am, she'll come here. She won't be able to help it. And they'll follow her.
(beat)
They'll use her to get to me. And then they'll kill her too.

JACK
You don't know that.

MARA
I watched them shoot two men like they were switching off lights.

Silence. The bulb hums.

MARA (CONT'D)
Promise me.

Jack looks at her for a long moment. Twenty years of silence sit behind his eyes.

JACK
I promise.
