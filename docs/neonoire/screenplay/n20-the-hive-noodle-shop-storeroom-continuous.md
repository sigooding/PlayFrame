NOBODY'S WITNESS
SCREENPLAY — SCENE 20 — INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM

INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Mara Voss.
Grammar: 35mm for the storeroom and the doorway; 85mm on the replayed voicemail and on the clip resting on the sketchbook. Nothing longer, and no knife.

INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - CONTINUOUS #20#

Flour sacks. The futon. The bare bulb.

MARA sits on the futon with her knees up and her phone to her ear, listening.

VERA (V.O.)
(filtered)
You left your umbrella. You'll get soaked.
(beat)
Just call me. You don't have to say anything. Just call, so I know.

The message ends. She plays it again.

Jack is in the doorway. She scrambles back against the wall.

JACK
My name is Jack. Your sister hired me.

MARA
Prove it.

Jack sets her sketchbook on a flour sack between them, with the hair clip on top.

Mara stares at it.

JACK
She says it was hers. She says you stole it when you were kids and never gave it back.

Something in Mara's face gives way.

MARA
It was never hers. Dad gave it to me. She just says that.

She pulls the sketchbook into her lap.

She almost laughs, and then she is crying, silently, pressing the back of her wrist against her mouth.

Jack waits. He doesn't go to her. He sits down on a flour sack, slowly, making himself smaller.

MARA
Is she okay? Is she -- does she know you're --

He doesn't answer. So she asks again.

MARA
Is she okay?

JACK
She's been calling you. She hasn't slept.

MARA
I know. I listen to them.

JACK
Why were you in Kanda, Mara?

She looks at him. Decides.

MARA
He wrote to Vera. Mr. Sakai. She threw it in the bin without even answering. I took it out.
(beat)
I called him. He said he had Dad's papers. He said to come to the bar at one o'clock. There'd be a man from a newspaper there too, so it couldn't be buried again.
(beat)
I sent them both a photo so they'd know me. He wrote back one line. "You have his face."

JACK
Vera doesn't know you went.

MARA
We had a fight about it. I called her a coward.
(small, bitter laugh)
And I'm the one hiding in a storeroom.
(beat)
Don't tell her I'm here.

JACK
She's your sister.

MARA
They have my purse. My passport. They know my name. They know where I live, where she lives, all of it. If she knows where I am, she'll come here. She won't be able to help it. And they'll follow her.
(beat)
They'll use her to get to me. And then they'll kill her too.

JACK
You don't know that.

MARA
I watched them shoot two men like they were switching off lights.

Silence. The bulb hums.

MARA (CONT'D)
Promise me.

Jack looks at her for a long moment. Twenty years of silence sit behind his eyes.

JACK
I promise.
