INT. ROADSIDE INN, BATHROOM - CONTINUOUS #42#

Dark. Steam still hanging. Jack climbs onto the edge of the deep tub and hits the swollen window frame with the heel of his hand. Once. Twice. It won't move.

Boots in the corridor.

The third time, he hits it with everything he has. The frame cracks open. Rain pours in.

He hauls himself through.
