EXT. THE PLAZA, WHERE THE HIVE WAS - DAY #99A#

Months later. Winter light, thin and clear. No rain.

A plaza of pale paving, exactly as it was in the model. A fountain, running. Small white trees in steel guards. Glass towers on three sides reflecting other glass towers. It is very clean.

At the edge, a hoarding: KUROSE DEVELOPMENT. TOMORROW'S TOKYO.

Nobody is in it.

A GARDENER in a company jacket crosses to one of the little trees and straightens it with the tip of his finger. It didn't need it.

Overhead, an elevated train passes. Nothing trembles.
