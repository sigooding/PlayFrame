EXT. VERA'S APARTMENT BUILDING, CORRIDOR - DAWN #78#

Grey light. An open-air walkway along the third floor. Rain dripping from the railing.

Jack at Vera's door. He doesn't knock. He sets the envelope on the mat, squares it to the door, and goes.

Vera opens her door. She is still in the dress. She hasn't slept.

On the doormat: a plain envelope. No name.

She picks it up. Opens it. The cloth-covered notebook. And tucked inside its cover, a folded sheet of lined paper.

She sees the name inside the cover, and her whole body goes still.

DANIEL VOSS.
