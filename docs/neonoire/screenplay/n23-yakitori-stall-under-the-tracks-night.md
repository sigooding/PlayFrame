EXT. YAKITORI STALL, UNDER THE TRACKS - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Detective Ishida, Jack.
Grammar: 35mm across the table, lanterns behind.

EXT. YAKITORI STALL, UNDER THE TRACKS - NIGHT #23#

Smoke. Red paper lanterns. Salarymen at plastic tables under a railway bridge, talking too loud.

Ishida sits alone at a corner table with a beer and a plate of skewers. Jack sits down opposite him.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
You never call me unless you want something.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Sakai.

Ishida sighs. Turns a skewer over with his chopsticks.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
You already know what he was. In the bubble years he cleared land. Old tenants who wouldn't sell. First he'd bring flowers. Then he'd bring men. Then something would happen. A fire, sometimes.

JACK
(in Japanese)
The Shiohama fire.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Among others.
(beat)
The journalist in the bar was from the Toto Shimbun. He'd been asking about the redevelopment money. Who owns what. Whose friends vote on what. The usual. Sakai called him a week ago. We think Sakai wanted to confess something before he died. He had cancer. Months left.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Confess what?

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Who knows. An old man's conscience. It's very common.

Jack watches him.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Why are you telling me all this?

Ishida puts down his chopsticks.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Because you'll find out anyway. And because I would rather you heard it from me than from someone who wants to hurt you.
(beat)
Twenty years ago I told you to sign that report. You were young. You did what your senior told you. That was on me, not on you.

JACK
(in Japanese)
I still signed it.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
And I've carried it too. Every day.
(beat)
Whatever Sakai hid, Jack, if you find it, bring it to me. Not to the department. To me. I'll know what to do with it this time.

A long pause. A train roars overhead. The lanterns sway.

Jack nods slowly.

It is exactly what he needed to hear. That is what makes it dangerous.
