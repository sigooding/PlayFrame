EXT. ROADSIDE INN, BACK YARD - CONTINUOUS #49#

The truck lurches forward and smashes through the farm gate. Two masked men throw themselves aside. The truck fishtails through the mud onto the farm track, then onto the highway.

The eight men walk out into the lot, one by one, and stand in the headlights of their cars, watching the single red taillight shrink into the rain.

They don't chase him. They don't need to.

The MASKED LEADER touches his earpiece.

MASKED LEADER
(in Japanese)
He's gone. He has what she gave him.

Behind them, inside the lobby, the pink payphone is still ringing.
