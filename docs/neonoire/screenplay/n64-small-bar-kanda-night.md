INT. SMALL BAR, KANDA - NIGHT #64#

Okada's bar. Late. No customers. The CRT television is off.

Jack slides the cassette case across the counter.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Keep this for me. Don't tell anyone. Not the police. Not me, if I ask you with someone standing behind me.

Okada looks at the tape. Then he takes it, lifts the lid of the old cash drawer, removes the tray, and puts the tape underneath.

OKADA
(in Japanese)
Behind the counter. Like the girl's clip.

OKADA (CONT'D)
(in Japanese)
The girl?

He pours Jack a drink. Jack doesn't drink it.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Safe. Tomorrow it'll be over.

OKADA
(in Japanese)
You said that about my daughter.
