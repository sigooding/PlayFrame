NOBODY'S WITNESS
SCREENPLAY — SCENE 98 — EXT. ROOFTOP OF JACK'S BUILDING

EXT. ROOFTOP OF JACK'S BUILDING - DAY
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Vera Voss.
Grammar: 24mm for the roof and the trains, 85mm on the clip, 50mm at the railing.

EXT. ROOFTOP OF JACK'S BUILDING - DAY #98#

Days later. The rain has stopped for the first time in the film. The sky is pale and washed and enormous.

A small flat roof: laundry poles, a water tank, a single potted plant someone has kept alive. Below, the elevated railway, and the trains going past at eye level.

Jack stands at the railing. He hears the door behind him.

Vera. In a plain coat. Her hair dry.

She comes to stand beside him at the railing. Not close. Not far.

VERA
Harada says the prosecutors want you to testify.

JACK
Yes.

VERA
And your licence?

JACK
Gone.
(beat)
It should have gone twenty years ago.

VERA
Ishida left a statement. He acted alone.

JACK
Is that what it says?

VERA
That's what the police say it says.

VERA
Harada has Mara's drawings. The pages from the floor of the bar. She says they're the only eyewitness there is.

JACK
She'd have liked that. Being asked.

VERA
Nobody asked her. She drew it anyway.

A train passes below. They watch it.

Jack takes something from his pocket. Holds it out on his open palm.

The red bird clip.

JACK (CONT'D)
This is yours.

Vera looks at it. She takes it.

JACK (CONT'D)
She said your father gave it to her. She said you only claim it was yours.

Vera laughs. It comes out broken.

VERA
Liar. It was mine.

She closes her hand around it.

Jack looks at the trains. It is the hardest thing he will ever say, and he says it simply.

JACK
At the end, she asked me to tell you something.

Vera goes very still.

JACK (CONT'D)
She said to tell you she was sorry.

Vera stares at him.

And then she understands. Not the words. What they mean. The fight. The last cruel things she said at the door. The flight home. Everything she has carried since the first scene.

Mara wasn't angry. Mara had forgiven her the whole time.

Vera turns away to the railing. Her shoulders shake. Jack doesn't touch her. He stands beside her while she cries, the way he knelt beside her in the rain. Close enough. Not closer.

When she can speak:

VERA
You could have told me that in the rain.

JACK
I couldn't say anything in the rain.

VERA
No.
(beat)
Neither could I.

A train. They watch it pass.

VERA (CONT'D)
I don't know what we are.

JACK
Neither do I.

VERA
I still have your lighter.

JACK
I know.

She takes it from her coat pocket. Looks at it. Opens it. Closes it.

She doesn't give it back. She puts it away again.

VERA
Kaneko's opening a new place. Near the station, under the arches. She's taking the sign. And the stools.
(beat)
She says the third one's still mine.

She walks to the rooftop door. Stops. Doesn't look back.

VERA (CONT'D)
She says she opens at six.

She goes.

Jack stands alone at the railing. A train passes, its windows full of people. For a moment, in one of the windows, the reflection of his own face.
