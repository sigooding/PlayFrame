INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Mara Voss, Kaneko.
Grammar: The warmest light in the Hive, on two faces and a parcel.

INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - CONTINUOUS #68#

Mara puts on a borrowed coat. She has a small cloth bag: a toothbrush, the cardigan, the sketchbook pages she tore from the wall.

Kaneko stands in the curtain. She hands Mara a small parcel wrapped in newspaper.

KANEKO
(in Japanese)
Rice balls. For the car.

Mara takes them. She doesn't know what to say.

MARA
(in halting Japanese)
Thank you. For --

KANEKO
(in Japanese)
Come back with your sister. Sit on the stools. Both of you. Eat everything.

Mara bows, too deep, clumsy. Kaneko, unexpectedly, puts a hand on her head. Just for a second. Like a grandmother.

JACK (O.S.)
It's time.
