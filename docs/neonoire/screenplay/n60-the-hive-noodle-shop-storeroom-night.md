INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Mara Voss.
Grammar: 35mm across the futon; the key changes hands for the last time.

INT. THE HIVE, NOODLE SHOP STOREROOM - NIGHT #60#

The bulb. Mara sits on the futon with her knees pulled up. Her face is swollen from crying. On the wall beside her, taped up with rice-paper tape: a new drawing, still smudged. The noodle counter from behind the curtain. The back of a head on the third stool.

Jack crouches in front of her.

MARA
She was right there. She was right there and I --

JACK
You did the right thing.

MARA
Did I?
(beat)
She said she'd bring me here. When she finds me. She was so happy.

She wipes her face with the heel of her hand.

JACK
If she found this place, other people can.

MARA
I know.

She opens her hand. The key. Its worn tag reads 114.

MARA (CONT'D)
Find what it opens. And then this ends. I don't want to hide any more. I want my sister.

Jack takes the key.

JACK
Tomorrow night I'm moving you out of here. Somewhere safe, outside Tokyo. A police car and a house. Someone I trust.

MARA
And then?

JACK
And then I tell her everything.

Mara looks at him. She believes him.
