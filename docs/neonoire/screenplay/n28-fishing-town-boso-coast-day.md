EXT. FISHING TOWN, BOSO COAST - DAY #28#

Grey sea. Grey sky. Boats pulled up on a concrete slipway. A single road of low houses, their paint scoured by salt wind.

Jack's old car, parked on the sea wall. He gets out, pulls up his collar against the wind, and walks.
