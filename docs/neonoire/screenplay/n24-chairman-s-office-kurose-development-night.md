Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading. Written, not boarded: the numbered shot board covers the opening scenes only.
Cast: — (not boarded; the draft names its own cast).
Grammar: Tokyo as a memory that is still happening. The city lights the characters, not the sky: vending machines, shop signs, train windows, fluorescent 
tubes. Sodium orange against a sick fluorescent green. Soft halation around every light, blacks slightly crushed. It should look like film, and feel 
like something remembered. Rain is never glamorous — no lightning, no storms, cold, steady, patient rain that turns the streets black and reflective. 
Wide and patient; close-ups are rare, so they count. Nothing is explained.

INT. CHAIRMAN'S OFFICE, KUROSE DEVELOPMENT - NIGHT #24#

The fortieth floor. Glass on three sides. Tokyo spread out below in the rain like a circuit board.

Silence. Thick carpet. A single orchid.

In the centre of the room, under a glass case: an architectural model of the redevelopment. White towers, green parks, tiny white trees. Where the Hive stands now, there is a plaza with a fountain.

THE CHAIRMAN, KUROSE (70s), stands at the window with his hands behind his back. Silver hair. A beautiful suit. The stillness of a man who has never had to hurry.

Behind him, near the door, stands the MASKED LEADER, unmasked here: a man in his forties in a plain dark suit, a face you would forget immediately. He is the man who touched his earpiece in the rain.

MASKED LEADER
(in Japanese)
The detective went into the Hive twice. Once in the day. Once after the last train.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
And?

MASKED LEADER
(in Japanese)
We can't follow him in there. Too many people. Too many passages. They see everyone.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
Then don't follow him in there.

He turns from the window.

KUROSE (CONT'D)
(in Japanese)
Let the detective look. He was always very good at finding things.
(beat)
It's what he does afterwards that he's bad at.

He looks down at the model. At the little plaza where the Hive will be. He straightens one tiny white tree with the tip of his finger.
