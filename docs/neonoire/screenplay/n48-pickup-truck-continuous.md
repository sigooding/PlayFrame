INT. PICKUP TRUCK - CONTINUOUS #48#

The keys are in the ignition, exactly where she said.

He turns them. The engine coughs. Dies.

The windscreen cracks as a round goes through it.

JACK
(under his breath)
Come on. Come on.

He turns the key again. The engine catches. Roars.
