INT. JACK'S OFFICE - NIGHT #18#

Rain. The old television plays static. Jack sits in the dark, the sketchbook open on the desk.

He turns to the last page. Tucked inside the back cover: a letter, folded small, creased as though it had been crumpled into a ball and smoothed flat again.

He unfolds it under the lamp. Cheap lined paper. Careful, laborious English handwriting.

.INSERT - THE LETTER

"Miss Voss. Your father did not kill himself. I can prove it. I have kept his papers for twenty years. I am old and I am ill and I do not want to carry them any more. Please telephone." A phone number. The signature: T. SAKAI.

.BACK TO SCENE

The envelope folded inside it is addressed to VERA VOSS.

Jack reads the letter twice. Then he looks at the name on the envelope for a long time.

The black rotary phone RINGS.

He answers. Listens.

KANEKO (V.O.)
(in Japanese; filtered)
Come back. Alone. After the last train.

The line clicks dead.
