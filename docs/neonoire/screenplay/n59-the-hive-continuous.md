SCREENPLAY — SCENE 59 — EXT. THE HIVE

EXT. THE HIVE - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Vera Voss, Jack.
Grammar: 50mm in the wet street; 35mm across the INTERCUT to the silent house. Her glow, his scan, and a dial tone in a room where nobody is left to hear it.

EXT. THE HIVE - CONTINUOUS #59#

Rain. By the Hive's narrow entrance, under a tin awning, a green public payphone. JACK stands at it with the receiver to his ear. It's ringing.

INTERCUT WITH:

INT. MRS. SAKAI'S HOUSE - SAME

The kotatsu. The altar. The television on with the sound off. A teacup on its side on the tatami. The phone rings in the empty room.

EXT. THE HIVE

Vera comes out of the Hive's narrow entrance, glowing.

VERA
Jack! It's still here. The noodle shop from the photo. It's real. The same woman -- she remembered Dad. She remembered me.

Jack keeps the receiver to his ear. It rings.

VERA
And I went to the newspaper. Harada, the editor. She says Kondo told two people about the one o'clock. Her. And a detective, who promised to keep a car nearby.

JACK
Later.

VERA
Jack. A detective.

JACK
Later. Please.

It rings. Nobody answers. He hangs up.

VERA
When we find Mara, I'm bringing her here. She'll finally see it.

JACK
Yes.
