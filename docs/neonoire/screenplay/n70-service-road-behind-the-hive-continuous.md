NOBODY'S WITNESS
SCREENPLAY — SCENE 70 — EXT. SERVICE ROAD BEHIND THE HIVE

EXT. SERVICE ROAD BEHIND THE HIVE - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Mara Voss.
Grammar: Twenty metres of wet asphalt; the dread is posture and distance.

EXT. SERVICE ROAD BEHIND THE HIVE - CONTINUOUS #70#

Jack and Mara come out of a narrow back door of the Hive into the rain.

The grey sedan waits beside the pillars, twenty metres away. A DRIVER in a dark coat gets out and opens the rear door for them. Polite. Professional. He bows slightly as they come.

Jack walks Mara toward it. Halfway there.

DRIVER
(in Japanese; quietly; subtitled: "Excuse me.")
Shitsurei shimasu.

He says it the way you would to a passenger.

Mara stops dead.

She has heard those words before. From the floor of a bar, before two shots.

MARA
(whispering)
That's them.

JACK
What?

MARA
That's what he said. Before he shot him. "Excuse me."

The driver lifts his wrist to his mouth and murmurs into his sleeve, very quietly:

DRIVER
Position.

Jack looks at the driver. The driver looks at Jack. And in that look, both men understand.

Jack shoves Mara back toward the Hive door.

JACK
Run!

From the dark between the pillars: muzzle flashes. Suppressed shots, flat and ordinary.

Jack draws Mara down behind a dumpster. Rounds clang off the steel. The driver pulls a weapon from his coat.

Mara breaks for the Hive door. Five metres. Three.

A shot.

She stumbles. Keeps going. Falls through the doorway.

Jack goes after her, low, as bullets chew the doorframe above his head.
