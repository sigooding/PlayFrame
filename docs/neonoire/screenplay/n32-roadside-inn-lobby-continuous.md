INT. ROADSIDE INN, LOBBY - CONTINUOUS #32#

A small, warm, faded room. A pink public payphone on the wall. A glass case of souvenir keyrings nobody has bought in decades. A CRT television on a shelf showing a night baseball game, the crowd murmuring.

MRS. NODA (70s) comes out from the back, drying her hands. MR. NODA (70s) doesn't look away from the baseball.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Just one night?

JACK
(in Japanese)
Just one.

She hands him a heavy old key on a wooden tag.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Upstairs, at the end. The bath is shared, I'm sorry. I'll show you. The light doesn't work properly.
