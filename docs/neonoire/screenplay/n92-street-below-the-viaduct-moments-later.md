EXT. STREET BELOW THE VIADUCT - MOMENTS LATER #92#

Vera and Jack step off the last rung of the fire ladder into an empty street. Soaked. Breathing hard.

Behind them, the Hive: every window lit again now, every radio playing, every resident awake. In the distance, sirens.

Vera steps away from him. Just one step. Enough.

They look at each other in the rain. There is too much to say, and none of it can be said tonight.

VERA
Is Kaneko --

JACK
The Hive looks after its own.

She nods. She looks up at the building full of light.

VERA
Mara was safe here. Until us.

He has no answer to that. She doesn't need one.
