INT. KANEKO'S NOODLE COUNTER - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Kaneko, Jack, Vera Voss.
Grammar: 24mm counter, 50mm three-shot, 85mm for Kaneko's refusal. The shutter crashes, the tube goes off, and the gas flame is all that's left.

INT. KANEKO'S NOODLE COUNTER - CONTINUOUS #86#

Kaneko pulls the shutter down with a crash. She turns off the fluorescent tube.

KANEKO
(in Japanese; to Jack)
Up. Through the back. The stairs by the dentist. All the way to the roof.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Come with us.

KANEKO
(in Japanese)
I have lived here fifty years. They can come and find me.

She pushes them toward the back.
