INT. KANEKO'S NOODLE COUNTER - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Vera Voss, Kaneko.
Grammar: 35mm frontal to the counter, as always.

INT. KANEKO'S NOODLE COUNTER - CONTINUOUS #56#

Lunchtime. Steam. Three customers.

Vera sits at the counter.

Kaneko turns from the pot, sees her, and freezes. Just for a second. Then she goes on working.

VERA
(in Japanese)
I'm sorry, I don't know what to order. I was here when I was a child. With my father. He always sat here.

Kaneko looks at her for a long moment.

KANEKO
(in Japanese)
He always paid too much.

Vera's breath catches.

VERA
(in Japanese)
You remember him?

KANEKO
(in Japanese)
And you. You ate everything on his plate.

She sets a bowl of noodles in front of Vera. Vera looks at it. Her eyes fill.
