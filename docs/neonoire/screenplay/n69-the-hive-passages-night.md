NOBODY'S WITNESS
SCREENPLAY — SCENE 69 — INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGES

INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGES - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Mara Voss, The Radio Repairman.
Grammar: Bare bulbs and open doors; the Hive's other lives, seen once.

INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGES - NIGHT #69#

Jack leads Mara through the narrow corridors. Bare bulbs. Dripping pipes. The radio repairman looks up from his bench as they pass, and nods.

Doors open onto other lives. A family watching television. A man asleep. A girl practising the violin, badly.

Mara looks at all of it as if memorising it.

MARA
Vera would love this.

JACK
You can bring her.

MARA
When it's over, you'll tell her? Everything?

JACK
Tonight. I'm meeting her at ten.

She stops walking. Looks at him.

MARA
Meeting her?

JACK
For dinner.

Mara studies him. And then, for the first time, she smiles. A real one. Her sister's smile.

MARA
Oh. Oh, I see.

JACK
It's not --

MARA
It is. It's fine. It's good.
(beat)
She deserves someone who looks for things.

A train passes overhead. The building trembles. They walk on.
