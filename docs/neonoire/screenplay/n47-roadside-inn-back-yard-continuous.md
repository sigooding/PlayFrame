EXT. ROADSIDE INN, BACK YARD - CONTINUOUS #47#

Rain. Mud. The farm gate. The pickup truck.

A masked man comes round the side of the building. Sees him.

Jack runs. Gunfire kicks up mud behind him. He dives behind the truck. Bullets punch through the bodywork above his head, through the tailgate, through a wheel.

He yanks open the driver's door and pulls himself in, flat along the seat.
