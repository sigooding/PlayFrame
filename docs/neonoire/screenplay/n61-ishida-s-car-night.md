INT. ISHIDA'S CAR - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading. Written, not boarded: the numbered shot board covers the opening scenes only.
Cast: — (not boarded; the draft names its own cast).
Grammar: Tokyo as a memory that is still happening. The city lights the characters, not the sky: vending machines, shop signs, train windows, fluorescent 
tubes. Sodium orange against a sick fluorescent green. Soft halation around every light, blacks slightly crushed. It should look like film, and feel 
like something remembered. Rain is never glamorous — no lightning, no storms, cold, steady, patient rain that turns the streets black and reflective. 
Wide and patient; close-ups are rare, so they count. Nothing is explained.

INT. ISHIDA'S CAR - NIGHT #61#

Parked under an elevated expressway. Rain drumming on the roof. The dashboard glow.

Ishida behind the wheel. Jack in the passenger seat. Neither looks at the other.

JACK
(in Japanese)
I have a witness. From the bar.

A pause. Ishida's expression doesn't change at all. That is the tell, and only we know it.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
The sister.

JACK
(in Japanese)
I need a safe car. A safe house outside the city. No paperwork. No department. Just you.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
When?

JACK
(in Japanese)
Tomorrow night.

Ishida nods slowly, as if thinking it through. As if it costs him something.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Nine o'clock. The service road behind the Hive, by the railway pillars. A grey car. My driver. I'll arrange the house myself.
(beat)
And Sakai's thing?

JACK
(in Japanese)
I'll bring it to you. When she's safe.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Good.
(beat)
You've done well, Jack. This time you've done well.

Jack looks out at the rain. For the first time in twenty years, he lets himself believe it.
