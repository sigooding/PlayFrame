INT. THE HIVE, STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Vera Voss.
Grammar: 24mm up the stair in the dark; 85mm on the hands. The building's noise is sound, not picture.

INT. THE HIVE, STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS #89#

Pitch black. Vera and Jack climbing a narrow concrete stair by touch. Her hand on the wall. His hand finding hers in the dark.

She lets him.

Below them, a flashlight beam sweeps the bottom of the stairwell. They freeze.

Somewhere a DOG starts barking. Then a baby cries. Then a radio, somewhere, turned up very loud: an old ballad. The one from the neighbour's wall.

Then another radio. Then a television. Then someone banging a pot with a spoon.

The whole building starts to make noise, on purpose, all at once. The flashlight swings away, confused.

Jack and Vera climb.

On a landing, a door opens a hand's width. An old woman's face, lit from below by a torch of her own. The one who was asleep in front of the sumo. She says nothing. She steps back and holds the door.
