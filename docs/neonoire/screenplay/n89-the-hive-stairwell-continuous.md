INT. THE HIVE, STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS #89#

Pitch black. A narrow concrete stair.

Vera climbs by touch, one hand on the wall. Her other hand finds Jack's and pulls him up after her.

Halfway up, she stops. Takes the lighter out of her pocket. Her thumb on the wheel.

Below them: boots. A flashlight beam crawls up the wall and falls away.

She puts the lighter back unlit, and climbs.
