EXT. ROADSIDE INN - NIGHT #31#

Two storeys of weathered wood and a tin roof, set back from the highway behind a gravel lot. A beer vending machine glowing by the entrance. A faded sign. The kind of place that was modern once, in about 1975.

A pickup truck, older than the building's paint, is parked crooked behind it.

Jack pulls into the lot. The only car.
