Grammar: Tokyo as a memory that is still happening. The city lights the characters, not the sky: vending machines, shop signs, train windows, fluorescent 
tubes. Sodium orange against a sick fluorescent green. Soft halation around every light, blacks slightly crushed. It should look like film, and feel 
like something remembered. Rain is never glamorous — no lightning, no storms, cold, steady, patient rain that turns the streets black and reflective. 
Wide and patient; close-ups are rare, so they count. Nothing is explained.

INT. BLACK SEDAN, BACK SEAT - CONTINUOUS #94#

Warm amber light. Leather. The rain is suddenly very far away.

Kurose sits in the back, impeccable, a small steel flask in his hands. He does not look at Ishida. He pours tea into the flask's cup.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
Get in, Ishida. You'll catch cold.

Ishida gets in. The door closes. The sound of the rain becomes a soft drumming on the roof.

The car pulls away.

Kurose offers him the cup. Ishida looks at it.

KUROSE (CONT'D)
(in Japanese)
It's only tea.

Ishida takes it. Doesn't drink.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
I did everything you asked. For twenty years.

KUROSE
(in Japanese)
Yes. You were always very careful.
(beat)
That's why I trusted you. And that's why you understand.

Ishida looks out of the window. Tokyo slides past in the rain: a lit vending machine, a closed noodle shop, an elevated train full of sleeping strangers.

ISHIDA
(in Japanese)
Where are we going?

Kurose doesn't answer. He simply looks ahead, as if the question was impolite.

Ishida nods, slowly. He understands.

He lifts the cup, and drinks the tea.
