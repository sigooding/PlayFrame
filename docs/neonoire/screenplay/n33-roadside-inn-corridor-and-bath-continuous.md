INT. ROADSIDE INN, CORRIDOR AND BATH - CONTINUOUS #33#

She leads him down a narrow wooden corridor. Past the kitchen: pots, a steel counter, a back door with a bolt.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Kitchen. If you want tea, just take it. The back door sticks. My husband says he'll fix it. He's said so since the Olympics.

JACK
(in Japanese)
Which Olympics?

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese)
Exactly.

She opens a door: a small tiled bathroom, a deep wooden tub, one high window over it, fogged with steam and swollen shut in its frame.

MRS. NODA (CONT'D)
(in Japanese)
If it's too steamy, the window opens. If you hit it hard enough.

Jack notices the window. He notices everything. That is the point of the scene, and nobody in it knows it.
