INT. ROADSIDE INN, JACK'S ROOM - NIGHT #34#

Tatami. A low table. A thin futon already laid out. Rain loud on the tin roof.

Jack sits at the table with Sakai's receipts spread out in front of him under the lamp. KATO RENTAL LOCKERS - UENO. No. 114. Twenty years of Januaries.

He takes out his phone. No signal.
