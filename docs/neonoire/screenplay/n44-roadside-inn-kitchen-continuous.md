INT. ROADSIDE INN, KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS #44#

-- through the open back door into the kitchen, where Mrs. Noda is crouched on the floor behind the steel counter, rigid with fear.

He grabs her arm, pulls her low toward the pantry, opens it, puts her inside.

JACK
(in Japanese; whispered)
Stay. Whatever you hear.

MRS. NODA
(in Japanese; whispered)
My husband --

JACK
(in Japanese; whispered)
I'll get him.

He closes the pantry door on her.

He moves up the corridor toward the lobby, keeping low along the wall.
