INT. PICKUP TRUCK - MOVING - DAWN #50#

Grey light. Empty highway. Rain through the shattered windscreen.

Jack drives with one hand. Blood runs from his hairline down the side of his face. His shirt is dark with it at the shoulder.

He looks in the mirror. No one.

He reaches into his jacket. The receipts are still there, soaked and creased.

He breathes. Only now does his hand start to shake.

Then the thought arrives, and his face changes.

Only two people knew where he was going tonight. He counts them silently.

Ishida. He asked Ishida for the widow's address.

And Vera. He called her from a pink payphone and told her it was just an inn.

He drives on. The wipers beat. He doesn't want to think it. He thinks it anyway.
