SCREENPLAY — SCENE 71 — INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGE

INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGE - CONTINUOUS
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Jack, Mara Voss, Kaneko, The Radio Repairman.
Grammar: The floor of the passage, and a thousand windows waking above it.

INT. THE HIVE, PASSAGE - CONTINUOUS #71#

Jack drags the door shut behind him and throws the bolt. Pounding on the door. Shots through it, splintering the wood.

Mara is on the floor of the narrow passage, her back against the wall. She is holding her side. Her hand is dark and wet.

Jack kneels, presses his hands over hers.

JACK
Stay with me. Mara. Stay with me.

Above them, a window opens. Then another. Doors bang open along the passage. Voices. Lights snap on, one after another, up through the whole building: the thousand windows of the Hive waking.

The RADIO REPAIRMAN runs into the passage with a flashlight. The FAMILY'S FATHER behind him with a baseball bat. Kaneko, in her apron.

Outside, a car door slams. An engine revs. Tyres on wet asphalt. Going.

And far away, finally, sirens.

Mara looks up at all the light.

MARA
Everyone's awake.

JACK
Yes. Everyone's awake. Help's coming.

Mara's hand finds his wrist. Her grip is weak.

MARA
Jack.

JACK
I'm here.

MARA
Tell her I'm sorry.

JACK
You'll tell her. Tonight. I'll bring her here, you can --

MARA
Tell her.

Her eyes stay on the lights above. Then they stop moving.

Jack doesn't let go of her hand.

Kaneko sinks down beside them in the narrow passage. She doesn't cry. She takes off her apron and folds it and places it gently under Mara's head, as though that still matters.

A train passes overhead. The bulbs swing. The whole building trembles, the way it always does.
