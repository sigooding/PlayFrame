Cast: Jack, Vera Voss.
Grammar: 50mm square to the stair, 85mm on the cup.

INT. JACK'S OFFICE - DAY #21#

Grey light. Rain on the window. Jack hasn't slept. He is in yesterday's shirt.

Footsteps on the stairs. The frosted glass. Vera's shape.

She comes in carrying two coffees in paper cups. She puts one on his desk.

VERA
You look terrible.

JACK
Thank you.

She sits. She doesn't take her coat off. She waits.

VERA
Did you find anything?

The sketchbook is on the desk between them. Jack's hand rests on its cover.

A train passes. The blinds shiver.

JACK
Not yet.

She nods. Believes him. That's the worst part.

VERA
Okay.
(beat)
Okay. Tomorrow, then.

She stands. At the door she hesitates, as though she wants to say something else. She doesn't. She goes.

Jack sits very still. He picks up the coffee she brought him and holds it without drinking, the way he held the can at the vending machine.
