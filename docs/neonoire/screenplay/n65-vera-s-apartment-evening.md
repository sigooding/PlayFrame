INT. VERA'S APARTMENT - EVENING
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Vera Voss.
Grammar: Wide and patient; the dress is the only saturated colour in the room.

INT. VERA'S APARTMENT - EVENING #65#

Rain on the window. The last grey light.

Vera stands on a chair and lifts down a flat cardboard box from the top of the wardrobe. Dust on the lid. Her mother's handwriting on the side, in marker: TOKYO.

She opens it on the bed.

Inside, folded in tissue paper that has gone yellow: a dress. Wine red. Silk. The kind of dress a young woman wore to an expensive restaurant in 1990 and never wore again.

Vera holds it up against herself in the mirror. For a moment she is someone else. Her mother, maybe.

Through the thin wall, the neighbour's radio begins to play the same old ballad from before: the woman's voice, the slow saxophone.

LATER:

Vera at the small mirror by the window, in the red dress, doing her makeup. She is out of practice. She wipes off the lipstick and does it again. Her hands aren't quite steady.

She looks at herself for a long time. She almost looks happy. She doesn't quite trust it.

She picks up her bag. Puts in her phone. Then, after a moment, the old steel lighter.

At the door she stops by the umbrella stand. The pale blue umbrella.

She takes it.

VERA
(quietly, to the empty room)
Borrowing it. You'll get it back.

She goes. Through the wall, the song plays on.
