NOBODY'S WITNESS
SCREENPLAY — SCENE 66 — INT. HOTEL LOUNGE

INT. HOTEL LOUNGE - NIGHT
Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
Cast: Vera Voss.
Grammar: Amber and olive, brass and bottles; the warmest room in the film.

INT. HOTEL LOUNGE - NIGHT #66#

An old hotel built in the Showa years for a future that never quite came. Amber light. Brass rails. Deep leather chairs. Bottles glowing behind the bar like a shrine.

In the corner, a PIANIST (60s) in a white jacket plays standards, softly, to almost no one.

A clock above the bar: 9:58.

Vera comes in, shaking the rain from the blue umbrella. People turn to look at her, briefly. She is beautiful and doesn't know where to put her hands.

She sits at the bar. Leans the blue umbrella against the next stool.

BARTENDER
(in Japanese)
For you?

VERA
(in Japanese)
I'm waiting for someone.
(beat)
A gin and tonic. Please.

She looks at the door.

The clock: 10:00.
