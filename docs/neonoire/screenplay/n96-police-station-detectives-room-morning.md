Source: the final screenplay (Neonoire (3).fountain, September 2026), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.

INT. POLICE STATION, DETECTIVES' ROOM - MORNING #96#

Grey daylight. A cardboard box on Ishida's desk. A young detective is clearing it.

He opens the bottom drawer. Empty.

The desk phone rings. He picks it up.

YOUNG DETECTIVE
Yes.
(beat)
Where?
(beat)
In his own car. Under the expressway. Sitting up, engine off. No marks.
(beat)
He left a statement.

He puts the phone down very slowly. He looks at the empty chair. At the empty drawer.

On the wall, the clock still runs a minute fast.
