# NEONOIRE — cast-sheet pass: nine identity sheets (26 September 2026)

**Scope:** nine identity sheets from ten generation calls. No shot was regenerated; no numbering moved.

## How the ten calls went

1–4. Kaneko, Okada, Kurose, Mr. Noda — installed as generated.
5–8. Mrs. Noda, repairman, Harada, young detective — Mrs. Noda, Harada and the young detective installed; **the repairman was not**: the first sheet dropped his round glasses and put him in a work jacket, described from memory instead of from the master. The house rule is attach, never describe; the sheet was discarded.
9. Repairman retaken from `s87/124` alone — glasses and grey knitted cardigan restored. Installed.
10. Vera's Look F sheet, from the retaken scene-100 master and her face sheet. Installed.

## Locks

- Every sheet is 16:9, 1920×1080, asserted in `scripts/verify-neonoire.mjs` by name.
- The sheets do not replace scene masters: a sheet carries the face and the garment; the room, the light and the staging still come from each scene's master (`s86/123` for Kaneko at the pot, `s82/104` for the newsroom, `s87/124` for the shop, `s46/213` for the pantry).
- Board paragraphs that said "has no sheet" (n82 Harada, n87 the repairman, n96 the young detective, n100 Look F) now name the sheet; Mr. Noda keeps **no cast card** — a sheet is not a card, and the cast count is locked at 18.
