- **291:** the handwritten bottle prices have drifted from shot 290.
- **292:** the camera sits higher than shot 24; she crouches in the aisle beside the crates rather than fully behind the counter flap.
- **293:** the box is in his left hand, not buttoned under the coat; the sedan's lamps read on.
- **294:** the parlour is a concrete vault, not the brick arch of 293; Jack is seen from behind.
- **295:** the sedan sits across the mouth rather than crawling the kerb; the sleeve-radio is not readable.
- **296:** the cassette is seen from the top and SHIOHAMA does not read; a pair of glasses hangs from his fingers, not in the draft; the coat sits more open than in 293.
