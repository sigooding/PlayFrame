## What good looks like

The studio keys in `public/images/neonoire/keys/` are the bar: romantic melancholy, rain reading as rain, practical light doing all the work, blacks crushed but never muddy, faces lit by the city rather than by a fill light, and figures small in patient frames. Quiet is the default; violence is rare, flat and unglamorous when it punctures the stillness.
