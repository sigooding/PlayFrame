# NEONOIRE — scenes 18–23, first boarding: the call after the last train (25 September 2026)

**Scope:** scene 18 (Jack's office, night), 19 (Kaneko's counter past midnight), 20 (the storeroom standoff), 21 (Jack's office, day), 22 (the brick arch counter under the tracks) and 23 (the yakitori stall under the bridge). **Nine shots, IDs neonoire-shot-191 … 199**, 16:9 full-bleed 1920×1080 JPEG, from **ten image-generation calls: six scene masters, one edit that stood Mara up, and three inserts**. Shots are numbered in boarding order, after the Hive first seen.

**Method, following the handoff's consistency note:** each scene's master was generated with the cast sheets and the room masters attached, and every other shot was derived from its master or its room. Every image was reviewed at full size against the **standing perspective check**.

## Locks

- **Jack's office** follows `s10/164-depends-whos-calling.jpg`: same desk, filing cabinet, CRT television, sofa and frosted glass door with the hand-lettered JACK. At night every practical is off and the static is the lamp (shot 191).
- **Kaneko's counter** follows `s17/187-you-got-old.jpg`; past midnight the shutter is half down, the fluorescent tube is off and one bulb burns (shot 192).
- **The storeroom** follows `s13/179-eat.jpg` and `s84/115-the-storeroom.jpg`: flour sacks, the futon, the crate of onions, one bare bulb.
- **The red bird clip** follows `s1/03-mara-walks.jpg`: a lacquered red enamel sparrow. It is Mara's by right of scene 20, and Vera wears it in her hair at the end of the film.
- **The brick arch counter** (shot 197) is a new location and is now its own master: green-tiled arch, ticket machine by the door, clock over the door, yellowed menus, radio behind the counter.
- **The yakitori stall** (shot 199) is a new location master: red lantern run, charcoal smoke, plastic tables under the steel bridge.
- **Vera** wears her original look, `sheets/vera.jpg`, in scenes 21–22. **Jack** follows `sheets/jack.jpg`; **Kaneko** `s86/123-fifty-years.jpg`; **Detective Ishida** `sheets/ishida.jpg`, no tie.
- **The stairway motif** (shot 195): Vera climbs Jack's stairwell from a low, level camera square to the flight — the same grammar as the Hive stair (shot 187) and the inn stairs, after Tokyo Story.

## Caveats carried, all reviewed at full size

- **193:** the first study sat Mara on the futon; an edit stood her up against the far wall as written. Two sketches now hang on the back wall that `s13/179` does not show — keep them or strike them on set, but decide once.
- **195:** the two paper coffees Vera carries are not legible in her hands at full size; the climb and the frosted door carry the beat.
- **196:** Jack's hands read older than 48, and the window is rain-frosted glass without shot 164's venetian blinds.
- **197:** the clock over the door is too small to read 11:40; Vera and Jack sit turned towards each other rather than square to the counter.
- **198:** the cup sits mid-counter rather than trembling at the edge; the careful reach reads either way.
- **199:** the lantern run hangs along the stall's eave and the bridge steel only enters at the top right; Ishida's grey suit carries no tie, as intended.
