## Vera's costume Look D

- **The director's standing rule (this session):** every Vera costume change from here on gets a **new coat in a colour that fits the scene**.
- **Look D** (`sheets/vera-look-d.jpg`, from scene 97): a dark olive-green knee-length wool coat with horn buttons and a loose belt, a charcoal roll-neck, black trousers, black ankle boots, a plain black umbrella, and her hair loose to the shoulder with the fringe.
- **Why olive:** it is muted and grown-up against the white tent and the grey rain. It is her first colour since the red dress, and she is no longer running.
- **Her face** still follows `sheets/vera.jpg`.
- **Planned next looks:**
  - Scene 98 (days later, pale washed sky): Look E, a pale stone or camel coat.
  - Scene 100 (weeks later, night, warm bulb, the red bird clip): Look F, a new coat.
