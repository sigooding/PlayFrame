## The call account — thirty-five generations, said plainly

The session cap is ten generations a turn. Four turns were spent; the ledger accounts every call.

**Turn 1 — 2 October, ten calls (the plan's ten).** Text-only studies of 3, 6, 7, 9 and 328–333.
Kept 3, 6 and 328. The other seven failed: the face-bearing frames drifted faces frame to frame
(cast-match failures), and 07 drew Mara at the doorway instead of the man.

**Turn 2 — 2 October, ten calls (re-draws).** Text-only second attempts at 7, 9, 329, 330, 331, 332
and 333, then the first face-crop studies of 330, 9 and 331 after the METHOD was found. Kept 329,
332 (no face in frame — text alone is safe there), 9 and 331 (face-crop, match the sheets). Thrown
away: every text-only face frame for drift, and the 330 face-crop, which drew the wrong subject.

**Turn 3 — 2 October, nine calls.** Face-crop re-draws of 330, 3, 6, 7 and 333; two stripped
subject-first attempts at 07 after it drifted again; and the first two scene-6 studies (321 the
form, 322 her handwriting) against the interview room master `s6/51`. 330, 3, 6 and 333 were
approved at review — **and then the workspace was snapshotted at 23:42 and the `artifacts/`
directory, which is gitignored, did not survive it.** The approved raws of 3, 6, 330 and 333, the
unreviewed sixth-attempt 07, and the two scene-6 studies were lost with it. The turn-1 text-only 3
and 6 stood installed as stopgaps — superseded the moment the face-crop studies existed, and
themselves lost when the replacements installed.

**Turn 4 — 3 October, six calls (the wrap).** 3, 6, 07, 330 and 333 re-drawn on the face-crop
method (07 on a subject-first brief with the street nouns stripped — the rich street brief kept
making the generator draw Mara at the doorway or the masked-men beat in the old man's place). All
five reviewed at full size and installed. One corrective redraw of 330 (330b) tried to lose its
second barber pole; it lost the half-shadow framing and the clip's single warm point instead, and
was rejected — 330 stands as drawn, with its caveat. Nothing was spent on anything else.

That is thirty-five calls for ten landed frames, and no frame on the board is a second-best the
director did not see the caveats for.

## Release semantics, said plainly

Following the house precedent (305–307, then 17/18 on 1 October): a pass that regenerates a frame
**and reviews it at full size** drops its `RETAKE PENDING` pin, because the marker's whole meaning
is "this study still shows a beat the rewrite retired".

- **3, 6, 7 and 9 are released** and read `Ready` — they are members of `coldOpenFreshCompleted`,
  so the builder derives the status from that list; the 30 September sign-off covers the
  composition and the pass, and **approval of the new pixels is the director's**. One line in
  `scripts/neonoire/rewrite-pending.mjs` re-pins any of them.
- **10 stays pinned** and is the next session's first call. `rewritePending` holds **10** frames
  (the nine older boards plus 10) where it held 14.
- **328–333 are Draft — production approval pending**, as fresh studies should be.
- Counts: **305 of 322 keyframes on disk**; the 17 placeholders left are scene 6's 321–327 and
  scene 14A's 334–343. Scene 1 is 22 of its 22 pictured.

Mechanical consequences, recorded so nobody has to re-derive them: `build-project.mjs` gained the
delivered-image branch for 328–333 (the placeholder text is for undelivered slots only);
`verify-neonoire.mjs` asserts the releases and their dates, the delivered six at 1920×1080 Draft,
the retired-beat absences (the recess hold, the old man approaching, the good coat, the pass-by,
the soft glow, the fall alone), and the placeholder lists; `src/lib/bundle-refresh.ts` carries the
pre-arrival note hashes for 328–333 so a saved workspace on the 2 October defaults adopts the

to "305/322 keyframes on disk, 17 to go".
