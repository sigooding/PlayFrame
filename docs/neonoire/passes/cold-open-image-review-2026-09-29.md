# Cold-open image review — scenes 1 and 2 (29 September 2026)

Reviewed image by image, at 960×540 in four-up sheets, in story order (the 21 frames of scene 1 and the 10 of scene 2). **Only these 31 frames were looked at**; the rest of the film was not re-reviewed for this note, only counted by status (below). Review sheet of the flagged frames, in this order — #07, #08, #10 / #12, #13, #17 / #20, #22, #27: `public/images/neonoire/reviews/cold-open-continuity-flags-2026-09-29.jpg`.

## Findings — none is fixed yet, all are retake work

**1. Mara vanishes from her hiding place (#08, #10, #12).** She is pressed into the barbershop's recessed doorway in #06 and #07 and again in #11, but in the frames between (#08 the sedan arrives, #10 the shot, #12 the masked man on the radio) the doorway is in shot and **empty**. The script keeps her there the whole time: she is in the recess until the car leaves. Retake with her back in the shadow of the recess, from the base frame plus `sheets/mara.jpg`.

**2. The car turns round in a lane too narrow for it (#08 → #13).** In #08 the sedan faces the camera, headlights on, at the far end. In #13, same camera, same lane, it shows its **rear with red taillights toward the camera**, well inside the lane. Between the two frames it has made a U-turn in a pedestrian alley about 2.5 m wide. The canon (`passes/kanda-alley-layout.md`) says the car never enters: it stops across the mouth and **reverses out**. A reversing car keeps its front to the camera and backs away with white reverse lamps. #13 should show that, at or near the mouth, not the rear inside the lane.

**3. The old man's body is missing in #13.** He is shot in #10 and lies in the lane in #14 and #18, but the empty lane in #13 has no body in it. (The board note says "no people"; the body is a person, and it should be there.)

**4. The bar sign and the car are at the same end (#07, #12, #13, #281).** Canon: the car end is at one end of the lane and the bar sign is at the *other*, and Mara runs away from the car toward the bar. In #12 and #13 the neon BAR is directly beyond the car, and #07 and #281 show the bar's sign at the end the car later comes from. #17 is right (she runs toward the camera, headlights behind her, no bar sign). The camera direction (barbershop left, vending machine right) is used for both ends, so the frames cannot all be right. Simplest fix: drop the BAR sign from #12 and #13 (and keep it only in the frames looking toward the bar end), or decide which end the car really is at and reshoot the odd ones out.

**5. The journalist's jacket changes (#20 → #22).** Dark charcoal jacket in #20; light tan jacket in #22 (and his face and haircut shift a little). Pick one wardrobe and retake.

**6. Loose shoes with no bodies (#27).** The floor of the bar shows a black shoe and a brown pair lying detached, with the journalist not in frame. Reads as an artefact. Retake or crop.

**7. Known, deliberately left:** #16 the key's tag reads **87**; the script (since 29 September) says **114** on the worn tag. The director chose to leave the old 87 frames rather than retake them. Carry as a known mismatch. Also #07 and four other scene-1 frames: the barber pole "reads slightly lit" (the master keeps it unlit).

Scenes 1 and 2 otherwise hold together: the lane's dressing (barbershop, striped pole, vending machine, shutters) is stable, Mara's wardrobe and the red bird clip are consistent, the bar interior is one place, and #280–282 (the added coverage) sit correctly in story order.
