## Continuity and editorial decisions

- **369** is the drawing of scene 2's bar from floor level, now in the storeroom; keep the page's small
  **1:07** and **EXCUSE ME** annotations. No added handwriting or person.
- **370** corrects the object logic from the old 258 study without changing or overwriting shot 258. The
  current script puts 114 on the tag; shot 258 still holds the original 87-tag / 114-metal treatment as
  a historical image. Do not promote 87 to current story canon.
- **371–372** are complementary to shots 284 and 345, not replacements. Mara wears the brown cardigan;
  her hair has no bird clip. The red enamel sparrow is on the closed sketchbook, not in her hair.
- **373–374** remain in the same scene-27A daytime bar: rubber mat down, no umbrella, CRT off. The 374
  reflection is one woman and her reflected face, not another person, an active picture, or a blue glow.
- **375** uses `artifacts/neonoire/coverage-369-376/375-the-line-ends-retake-raw.jpg`. The first study,
  `375-the-line-ends-raw.jpg`, had incorrect lettering on a visible door and static on the CRT; it is not
  installed. The desk-only retake removes both continuity hazards. Shot 252's lighter action and its
  image remain intact.
- **376** is the Jack-side intercut assigned to scene 36 because that is where the line falls on the
  screenplay page. Vera's existing 210 remains at home; shot 209 remains the inn-wide master. Jack, Mr.
  Noda, the pink payphone and the inn's warm/green light stay consistent with scene 35.
