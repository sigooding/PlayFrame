## The decisions this round made (permanent unless the director overrides)

1. **The unboarded script beats are the pass's contribution.** The 4 October retake round recorded two
   beats the script names with no frame anywhere — scene 59's INTERCUT to Mrs. Sakai's empty house and
   scene 94's door closing. Neither is a row in the long-talking table, which is exactly why both
   same-day long-hold passes left them unboarded; they are this pass's five unique frames, 364–368.
2. **The INTERCUT is four cuts, not five.** The page writes five objects — the kotatsu, the altar,
   *the television on with the sound off*, the teacup, the phone — but a bare television screen has
   no business as its own still: the room wide already shows it on, glowing, and the empty room says
   everything a screen would add. So 364 carries the "sound off" beat and the series is four, all
   35mm across the cut on the scene's own grammar, playing before 228 in the page's order.
3. **The house is the scene 29 house with the people removed.** Mrs. Sakai is the widow of scene 29;
   "nobody is left to hear it" means the same room, the same drained interior — tatami beige, altar
   gold, grey daylight — emptied. The altar photograph is held to `s1/07-old-man.jpg` (the cold open's
   man, the one the film never names on screen), and 365 is the face 364's wide must agree with. The
   long-hold frames of scene 29 (354–357) are the house with its widow in it, that day; these four are
   the same house after she is gone.
4. **368 is the hinge, not the walk or the sit.** 142 owns the stop in the rain and 143 owns the
   sitting, so the door frame is the getting-in and the closing: 50mm at the door on the scene's
   lens plan, cream leather and amber carried from `s94/141-he-drinks.jpg`, the street and its one
   lit window carried from `s94/139-only-tea.jpg`, Ishida NO TIE and soaked.
5. **The rest of the table is queued.** The long-hold passes covered the table's first five rows
   (23, 22, 20, 29, 17, 14 — six rows in all); the next pass takes **scene 25's 202 and 258 first**,
   then scene 20's 284, scene 27A's 292 and 290, scene 11's 251, scene 36's 210, scene 10's 166, then
   the below-30 s list, the song (74–77), the rewrite-changed frames (64, 36, 70) and the
   title/credit cards — all still standing in [shots-needed.md](../shots-needed.md).
6. **Nothing was replaced, so nothing was backed up.** All five frames are new files at new numbers;
   no existing image moved. The backup rule is asserted vacuously: every pre-existing study is
   byte-identical to the commit before this pass except the bundle, the boards and the scripts it
   touched.
