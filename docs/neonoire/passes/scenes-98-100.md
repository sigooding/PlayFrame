## Vera's costumes: Looks E and F

- **The director's standing rule, as tightened this session:** every Vera costume change gets a **new coat**. That means a different garment and silhouette, not the same coat recoloured, in a colour that fits the scene.
- **Look E** (`sheets/vera-look-e.jpg`, scene 98): a **short hip-length boxy oatmeal car coat** with a high funnel collar and patch pockets, a pale sky-blue shirt collar, blue straight jeans, white trainers, and her hair dry. It is short and soft after four long coats, and pale for the film's first dry day.
- **Look F** (scene 100; there is **no sheet**, and shot 160 is its master): a **deep teal double-breasted wool peacoat**, hip length, worn with the red bird clip in her hair. The teal sits against the amber bulb so the red clip is the brightest thing in the frame.
- **Her face** follows `sheets/vera.jpg` in both.
