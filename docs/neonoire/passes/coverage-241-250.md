# NEONOIRE — coverage pass: the beats the first boarding named, shots 241–250 (26 September 2026)

**Scope:** the first boarding closed at 240 shots, one or more per scene. The boards themselves had already named the beats still missing. This pass adds those, **without renumbering 1–240**. Ten shots, IDs `neonoire-shot-241` … `250`, 16:9 full-bleed 1920×1080 JPEG, from **ten generation calls**. Four failed the beat and could not be retaken: the image service allows ten generations in a turn, and all ten had been spent. Those four are installed with the failure logged, not hidden.

**Method:** each shot was generated from its locked location master with the cast sheet attached. Lettering that had to be read (the card, the clipping, the phone) was composited after generation, the same way the CRT rule is applied, and cropped at full size before install. Numbers stay in boarding order. Coverage shots live on their own scene boards and are collected after shot 240 in `SCENES` order.

- **Vera** in 243 and 244 is the original look (`sheets/vera.jpg`): charcoal coat, cream knit, gold ring, pale-blue umbrella. No later costume look.

## Caveats carried — retakes, next turn

The generation limit was reached. These are logged, not claimed as clean:

- **241:** Jack's face matches the sheet and the train matches the rooftop, but the face reads as a man inside the carriage, not a glass reflection. Dress the reflection on the retake.
- **242:** Okada matches, and the CRT is correctly off, but his eyeline goes to the street window rather than down the counter to the mat. The soft figure in the doorway is not Vera; drop her.
- **243:** the umbrella is still in Okada's other hand; by this beat he has already handed it over. Katakana and the address are not lettered (no Japanese font this session).
- **245:** the office, the door (JACK), and the samurai film match, but his feet are on the floor. The script has him on a chair.
- **248:** a second barber pole reads in the deep background, the same flaw as shot 170. Keep one on set.
- **249:** Mara matches the sheet, no clip, and the screen is legible, but her eyes are on the camera, not the phone.
- **250:** five stools read, not six. Dress the sixth on set.
