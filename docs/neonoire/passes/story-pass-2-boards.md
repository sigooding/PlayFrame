# Story pass 2 — the boards and the retakes (29 September 2026)

The script half of story pass 2 landed with the bible's Part 9 item 7: the "Shitsurei shimasu" tell,
Mara's floor-level drawings, Vera's own errands, Jack owning his silence, the evidence chain, Ishida
found dead with a statement, Kurose taken away and the plaza built, and the quiet escape (scenes 90–91
rewritten in place). This pass is the board half — and the retakes that closed the rewritten scenes.
Ten generation calls in all, per the house rule. See the [handoff](../handoff.md) for the part-by-part
record and [`story-bible.md`](../story-bible.md) Part 9 item 7 for the reasoning.

## What changed on the boards

- **Scene 83 (folder, not notebook).** Shots 111 and 114 now board Vera with a plain folder of
  photocopies — the notebook itself is in the newspaper's safe since 82A — and she shows Kurose a
  single copied page. The installed studies still show the cloth-covered notebook: an honest prop
  discrepancy and a production retake check, logged in the board notes.
- **Scene 84 (the bag).** Shots 117 and 119 now board Mara's small cloth bag (one strap frayed) in
  Vera's hands, and 119 carries the evidence exchange: the bag opened on the flour sack, the floor
  drawing with EXCUSE ME, "She drew what she saw. All of it." / "I'm taking these to Harada." The
  installed studies predate the bag and the spread page — production checks, not blocked frames.
- **Scene 96 (the phone call).** New coverage **shot 303**: the young detective at Ishida's cleared
  desk takes the call — "Where?" / "In his own car. Under the expressway. Sitting up, engine off. No
  marks." / "He left a statement." Placeholder study (`s96/303-he-left-a-statement.jpg`).
- **Scene 97 (the crawl, Harada).** New coverage **shot 304** — the shop-window insert with Ishida's
  smaller photograph beside Kurose's and the crawl POLICE DETECTIVE FOUND DEAD. STATEMENT LEFT. — and
  shot 149 now boards Harada on Vera's other side (press card on a lanyard, held to
  `sheets/harada.jpg`). The installed 149 predates all three; logged as production checks.
- **Scene 53A boarded — `n53a-vera-at-the-toto-shimbun.md`, shots 297–299.** Vera at the Toto Shimbun
  alone with Kondo's card: the glass office, Harada's desk and the photograph ("There was no car."),
  and the card left with Vera's number on its back. Newsroom held to `s82/104-the-newsroom.jpg`; Vera
  in her original look (Look C begins at 83).
- **Scene 82A boarded — `n82a-the-notebook-photocopied.md`, shots 300–302.** Vera at the newsroom's
  edge with the notebook and the folder while Jack talks behind the glass; the photocopier's bar of
  light ("Turn. Press."); the warm folder handed over ("The copies. Not the original."). The hook —
  Kondo's redevelopment files, and the grey car registered to a Kurose security company — rides in
  shot 302's note into scene 83.
- **Scene 99A boarded — `n99a-the-finished-plaza.md`, shots 305–307.** The plaza built exactly as the
  model, the KUROSE DEVELOPMENT. TOMORROW'S TOKYO. hoarding, and the gardener straightening a tree
  with one finger while the same train passes and nothing trembles.

Nothing renumbered: the new shots sit in the story-pass-2 coverage block (297–307) and the six
inserted scenes keep their lettered numbers.
