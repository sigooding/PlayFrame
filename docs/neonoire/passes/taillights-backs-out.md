# NEONOIRE — shot 13 retake: the sedan backs out (27 September 2026)

**Scope:** one retake of shot 13, `s1/13-taillights-gone.jpg`. Nothing renumbered. Two generations were spent. The first faced the car the wrong way (headlights toward camera) and was not installed.

Review sheet: [taillights-backs-out.jpg](../../../public/images/neonoire/reviews/taillights-backs-out.jpg) — shot 8 beside the retake.

## Why it cannot turn around

Shot 8 stops the sedan nose-to-camera in an alley one car wide. The open doors already sit against the walls. A U-turn or a three-point turn does not fit. The rear already points down the lane, back the way it came. The only departure that shows taillights to this camera is a reverse.

The previous frame had the rear toward camera and red tails only, which read as a forward drive-away — a car that had faced about. That was the error.

## The retake

Same camera, same alley: barber pole and door on the left, vending machine on the right. The sedan backs straight out. Rear to camera, doors closed, not steered. White reverse lamps are lit with the red taillights, and both smear on the wet asphalt between the car and the camera. The doorway is empty. Mara does not move. No people.

## Caveats

- The plate is not legible. Do not claim a number.
- The vending face drifted slightly from the master. The pole, the door and the side hold.
- Scene 95 (`s95/142-taillights.jpg`) was not retaken. Its light trail still draws a U-turn. The script there asks for a corner, on a different street, and that frame is a separate question.
