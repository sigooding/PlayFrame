## Outro song (first version)
The author's song, first version (single voice, Cm / A# / G# / G). It plays over the end credits. Nothing else is scored yet.

Structure: Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Chorus, Bridge ("Time is all we have"), Chorus, Outro ("Welcome to the show"), with a `[screech sample]` cue twice.
Refrain: "Are you a hero, heroine or bystander? Don't be complicit to the lie."

Ties to the script: complicity (Jack signed the Shiohama report), Mara running from the bar, rain and the villain in the rain, "digging up my past", the twenty years.
