# Jack — character and visual continuity

## Canon, not the old placeholder

The final screenplay introduces **JACK (48)** in scene 10: “Handsome once, in a way he stopped maintaining. A good coat, badly kept.” His card says **JACK. INVESTIGATIONS**, no surname. He is a former police detective (scene 83), now a private investigator. The surname and nationality are not specified on the page.

**He is not the sisters' father.** The man in the family photograph is **Daniel Voss, 41**, identified by the newspaper clipping in scene 11. Daniel has a separate cast entry and the parent links. Jack's connection to Vera is attraction, trust and betrayal; to Mara, protection and a promise. Never give him the Voss surname or a parental relationship to either sister.
