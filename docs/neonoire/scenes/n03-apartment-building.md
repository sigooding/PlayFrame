# NEONOIRE — SCENE 3 — EXT. VERA'S APARTMENT BUILDING

**Board, and the script.** The screenplay page for this scene is [n03-apartment-building.md](../screenplay/n03-apartment-building.md), carrying the draft verbatim.

Scene: EXT. VERA'S APARTMENT BUILDING — DUSK
Cast: — (the building carries the scene; Vera's lit window is the only figure)
Lens plan: 24mm for the block, 35mm for the building against the elevated line, 85mm for the laundry and the lit window.
Grammar: dusk, not night, and the first time the film has been out in daylight of any kind — grey, wet, going blue. The city still lights the frame: one window on the third floor, train windows sliding past, sodium starting up below.

## Frame format — 26 September 2026 (retaken 29 September 2026)

All four shots (29–32) are rebuilt **16:9, 1920×1080, full-bleed** — 29–31 earlier on 26 September 2026, the laundry insert 32 later the same day, closing the last legacy frame in the film. **Retake 29 September 2026 (shots 29 and 30):** the earlier studies of 29 (`s3/29-apartment-block.jpg`) and 30 (`s3/30-the-elevated-line.jpg`) had been generated with `keys/06-the-block.jpg` (the Hive key) attached and drew a dark, pipe-and-cage-encrusted self-built block wedged between gleaming glass office skyscrapers — the Hive's silhouette — with the lit window jumping from the third floor in 29 to the fourth floor in 30. **Vera lives in an ordinary old four-storey apartment building somewhere else in Tokyo, beside the elevated railway — NOT the Hive.** The story depends on her *not* knowing the Hive until scene 55. Shots 29 and 30 are retaken against `s3/31-the-lit-window.jpg` and `s78/88-the-walkway.jpg` (`scripts/neonoire/apartment-look.mjs`, `apartmentBuildingLook`): a plain 1970s four-storey grey precast-concrete residential block squeezed between two ordinary newer five-storey beige tiled residential manshon buildings on a quiet wet Tokyo street, external steel staircase on the left, standard balconies on the right, bicycles at the foot of the wall, the concrete elevated railway viaduct directly behind, and **only one window on the third floor lit** with warm amber table-lamp light across 29, 30 and 31. The `THREE DAYS LATER` super in shot 29 is a title, not photography: it is added in the grade and never baked into the keyframe.
