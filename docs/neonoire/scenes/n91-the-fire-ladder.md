# NEONOIRE — SCENE 91 — EXT. THE HIVE, REAR WALL BY THE VIADUCT — CONTINUOUS

**Board, and the script.** The screenplay page for this scene is [n91-the-hive-rear-wall-by-the-viaduct-continuous.md](../screenplay/n91-the-hive-rear-wall-by-the-viaduct-continuous.md), carrying the draft verbatim.

Scene: EXT. THE HIVE, REAR WALL BY THE VIADUCT — CONTINUOUS
Cast: JACK, VERA VOSS (the masked men only as a flashlight beam at a window)
Lens plan: 24mm on the iron ladder against the viaduct wall; 50mm on her hand in his.
Grammar: a train arrives overhead, a roaring wall of lit windows, and under it they descend the ladder unheard. Her hand is in his and at the bottom she doesn't take it back. This is the one train shot kept from the old sequence, as cover.

## Rewritten and retaken 29 September 2026

The old railway-walkway scene is cut.
