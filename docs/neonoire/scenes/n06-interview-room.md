# NEONOIRE — SCENE 6 — INT. POLICE STATION, INTERVIEW ROOM

**Board, and the script.** The screenplay page for this scene is [n06-interview-room.md](../screenplay/n06-interview-room.md), carrying the draft verbatim.

Scene: INT. POLICE STATION, INTERVIEW ROOM — MOMENTS LATER
Cast: VERA VOSS, DETECTIVE ISHIDA (50s)
Lens plan: 24mm for the room, 35mm for the two-shot across the table, 50mm for each of them, 85mm for the tea, the card and the long looks.
Grammar: one table, two chairs, rain on a frosted window. The scene is a two-hander watched from a third chair. Ishida's English is excellent and Vera refuses it: she answers in Japanese, and the subtitles carry the scene. Nothing in this room is violent; everything in this room is a man deciding how much to say.

Vera answers in Japanese: "I speak Japanese." A small beat. He accepts it with a mild smile: of course, but this is a difficult thing, it is easier in your own language. She lets it go.

58. TWO-SHOT — 35mm, static, eye level — three days ago.
"When did you last see your sister?" — "Three days ago. At my apartment. We had dinner." — "And how did she seem?" Vera hesitates. "Normal."

NOTE: The scene's one true lie by omission, and the audience already knows what Vera does not: her sister is alive, hiding behind a bar counter in Kanda. Play the hesitation as embarrassment, not guilt.

60. MEDIUM — 50mm, static, eye level — Kanda.
"Did she ever mention Kanda? Friends there? Work?" — "Kanda? No. Why Kanda?" — "We ask about many places." — "You asked about one."

NOTE: Her third line in a row is a cornering, and he enjoys it: the only light moment in the room. Kanda is the street the audience has just been in, and nobody in the film will say why.
