# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 5 — THE NIGHT AT PAT'S (revised)

Scene: EXT./INT. PAT'S ROAD AND HOUSE — NIGHT
Cast: KATH (30s), RAY (30s), DANNY (40), JODIE (11), PAT (70s), MALCOLM (70s), NEIL (50s, a blank, talks about flooring)
Grammar: No CCTV anywhere. Two grammars only. The cops: static two-shot through the windscreen, 32mm locked off, handheld only for violence. Danny and Jodie: handheld, close, dark, red torchlight (bike light, torch with red filter). The demons are only ever seen in someone's torch beam — never in their own grammar. Dry, deadpan British black comedy, never grief or horror performance. No score, no reaction inserts.

This replaces the previous version of Pat's house. No surveillance, no 4:3, no timecode. The water bottles by the gate are the connective tissue from the scout hut.

---

1. INT. POLICE CAR — STATIC TWO-SHOT, 32mm, locked off, through the windscreen, night
Parked across the road from Pat's. Lights off. Kath has a family bag of crisps. Ray has nothing. The single bottle of water in the cup holder, still untouched.
KATH: This is a stakeout, this.
RAY: It's an obs.
KATH: It's a stakeout.
RAY: A stakeout's American.
KATH: (a beat) What's ours?
RAY: Sitting in a car.

2. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, exterior, locked off, from behind the car
The car, dark. Across the road, Pat's house, one lamp on. At the gate, lined up neatly on the pavement: six bottles of water.

3. STATIC TWO-SHOT — as 1
KATH: Them bottles have been there since teatime.
RAY: Mm.
KATH: Who leaves water on a pavement?
RAY: (thinking about it) Someone who's got a lot of water.
KATH: (writing it down) Suspicious.

4. EXT. PAT'S GATE — HANDHELD, 35mm, close, red bike light
Danny and Jodie at the gate. Danny crouched at the bottles, two already in his bag.
DANNY: Right. Grab them and go.
JODIE: I'm going in.
DANNY: You're not.
JODIE: There's someone in the cellar.

5. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
DANNY: How do you know?
JODIE: There's a door under the stairs and she never looked at it once. Nobody doesn't look at a door.

6. HANDHELD — 35mm, two-shot, very close
DANNY: Rules.
JODIE: Nothing off anyone who's still alive.
DANNY: Right.
JODIE: He's still alive. So we can't leave him. We'd be taking him off himself.
(Pause. Danny tries to work out whether that's how his rules work.)
DANNY: That's not what it means.
JODIE: (already opening the gate) It is now.

7. INT. HALL — HANDHELD, 35mm, close, red torchlight
Dark. The front door opens a crack. Two torch beams, one red, crossing the carpet.

8. HANDHELD — 35mm, the front room doorway, torch sweeping
The beam passes a doily, an armchair, a sideboard — and back. Pat, standing in the middle of the room in the dark, facing the fireplace. Not moving.

9. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
He's frozen. Every muscle.

10. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
She looks at Pat. She looks at her dad. She mouths: she's facing the fireplace.

11. HANDHELD — 28mm, low, following them along the hall
They creep past the front room doorway, one at a time. Pat in the back of frame, soft, facing away. She does not turn.

12. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, torch on the cellar door
The key in the lock. A Post-it in biro: DO NOT FEED.

13. HANDHELD — 28mm, the cellar door
Jodie turns the key. It's loud. Nobody breathes.

14. INT. CELLAR — HANDHELD, 35mm, red torch from the stairs
The beam finds a radiator, a garden chair, and chained to the radiator by a bike lock round the ankle, sat with perfect posture: NEIL. He squints into the light, pleasantly.

15. HANDHELD — 35mm, coming down the stairs
Jodie first. Danny behind her, torch shaking.

16. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on NEIL
Hands on his knees.
NEIL: Hello.
JODIE: (whispering) We're getting you out.
NEIL: (warmly) I can only apologise.
JODIE: For what?
(A genuine pause while Neil looks for an answer.)
NEIL: Generally.

17. INSERT — 85mm, handheld, red
The bike lock. A four-digit combination.

18. HANDHELD — 35mm
DANNY: We need bolt cutters.
JODIE: (already turning it) No we don't.

19. INSERT — 85mm, handheld
1 — 2 — 3 — 4. It opens.

20. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
DANNY: Who uses one two three four?
JODIE: Old people.

21. HANDHELD — 28mm
Neil stands. Slowly. Brushes down his trousers.
NEIL: (normal speaking volume, in a silent house) Is this solid concrete, do you know? Because you'd want a membrane before you put any laminate down.
DANNY: (hissing) Shh.
NEIL: (helpfully) Sorry. (a beat, same volume) Five mil at least.

22. HANDHELD — 35mm, up the cellar stairs, red torch swinging
The three of them coming up. Neil still talking.
NEIL: — people think it's the boards, but it's the underlay —

23. HANDHELD — 28mm, the top of the stairs
The torch comes up and finds a pair of slippers. Tilt up: Malcolm, in a dressing gown, in the cellar doorway. Holding a mug of tea.

24. HANDHELD CLOSE — 65mm, on MALCOLM
MALCOLM: (quietly furious) He was nearly there.

25. HANDHELD CLOSE — 65mm, on MALCOLM
The mouth opens. Too wide. The eyes go black. He's still holding the tea.

26. HANDHELD — chaotic, 28mm
Jodie goes under his arm. Danny goes into him shoulder first. The tea goes everywhere. Neil walks past the whole thing at his normal pace.

27. HANDHELD — 28mm, chaotic, down the hall
Three people running for the front door. Neil not running. The camera stuck behind him.

28. HANDHELD — 35mm, over Jodie's shoulder as she passes the front room
Her torch catches Pat. Body still facing the fireplace. Head turned all the way round to watch them go.

29. EXT. PAT'S FRONT PATH — HANDHELD WIDE, 28mm — first handheld for the cops
The front door bangs open. Danny, Jodie, then Neil, strolling. Across the road, both car doors open at once.
RAY: (entirely official) Police! Stop there please!

30. HANDHELD — 35mm, the gate, chaotic
Danny stops dead, because it's the police. Jodie doesn't, because it's the police. Neil stops because someone told him to.

31. HANDHELD — 35mm, close
Kath has the taser out.
RAY: Kath.
KATH: What.
RAY: Form.
KATH: (lowering it, annoyed) Fine.

32. HANDHELD — 28mm
Ray cuffs Danny against the police car.
DANNY: There's something in there.
RAY: You do not have to say anything —
DANNY: There's two of them —
RAY: — but it may harm your defence —
DANNY: (desperate) Their faces go.
RAY: (carrying on, sincerely) — if you do not mention when questioned something which you later rely on in court.

33. HANDHELD — 35mm
KATH: Stay there.
JODIE: Don't go in.
KATH: (kindly, to a child) We have to secure the premises, love.

34. INT. HALL — HANDHELD, 35mm, two torch beams
Kath and Ray come in. The front door swings shut behind them.

35. HANDHELD — 35mm, the cops' torches down the hall
The beams find the kitchen doorway, and in it, side by side: Pat and Malcolm. Cardigan. Dressing gown. Perfectly still.

36. HANDHELD — 50mm, two-shot on the cops
KATH: Evening. We've had reports of a disturbance.
PAT: (o.s., warmly) Oh, no, love.

37. HANDHELD CLOSE — 65mm, on PAT and MALCOLM, in torchlight
Pat has a knife and fork. Malcolm has a fish slice.
RAY: Sir. Madam. Put the cutlery down please.

38. HANDHELD CLOSE — 65mm
Both mouths open. Too wide. Both pairs of eyes go black. Neither of them has stopped smiling.

39. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on RAY
Four seconds. His face does nothing at all.
RAY: (flatly, to Kath) Out.

40. HANDHELD — chaotic, 28mm
Two police officers running out of a pensioner's house.

41. EXT. ROAD — HANDHELD, 28mm
Across the road at a sprint. Kath gets the back door open.
KATH: In! In! Everyone in!
Danny, still cuffed, bundled in. Jodie dives in after him. Neil gets in last and puts his seatbelt on.

42. HANDHELD — 35mm, through the windscreen
Ray in the driver's seat. Keys. It doesn't start. It starts.

43. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, from behind the car — same framing as shot 2
The car pulls away hard. In the lit doorway of the house, Pat, back to normal, cardigan straight, raising one hand.
PAT: (calling, pleasantly) Bring my bottles back, love!

44. INT. POLICE CAR — STATIC TWO-SHOT, 32mm, locked off, through the windscreen — their grammar, restored, five people in it now
Kath and Ray in the front. In the back, squashed: Danny in cuffs, Jodie, Neil with his seatbelt on. Nobody speaks.
Hold. Eight seconds.
KATH: That was drugs.
RAY: Some sort of condition.
KATH: Old people get conditions.
RAY: They do.

45. STATIC TWO-SHOT — as 44
DANNY: Am I still under arrest?
KATH: Yes.
DANNY: After that?
RAY: Can't un-arrest you. There's nobody to process it.
DANNY: So I'm arrested forever?
RAY: (considering it) Till something opens.

46. STATIC TWO-SHOT — as 44
Kath turns round in her seat. Looks at Neil.
KATH: You.
NEIL: Hello.
KATH: Say sorry.
NEIL: I can only apologise.

47. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on KATH
Genuinely delighted.
KATH: (to Ray) There.
RAY: That's one.
KATH: That's our first one.

48. INSERT — 85mm, static
The cup holder. Jodie's hand comes through from the back, takes the bottle, and unscrews it.

49. STATIC TWO-SHOT — as 44
Nobody stops her. Ray watches her drink it in the mirror.
RAY: That's evidence, that.
JODIE: (still drinking) Of what?
RAY: (long pause) I'd have to check.

50. STATIC TWO-SHOT — as 44
Hold. Five seconds.
NEIL: (pleasantly, to everyone) Have any of you ever thought about laminate?

51. EXT. ROAD — STATIC WIDE, 135mm long lens, locked off
The police car, tiny, headlights on, on an empty road.
Hold. CUT.
