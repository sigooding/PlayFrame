## EPISODE ONE — THE MUGGING

Scene: EXT. SIDE STREET — EARLY MORNING
Cast: THE OLD WOMAN (70s, good coat, handbag over her forearm), THE MUGGER (19, hood up, wired). Both unnamed, cast nowhere.
Grammar: the camera keeps operating. No flash, no sound, no score, no reaction cut. Sodium light and a glowing cashpoint, still dark. The violence is texture, not event. The title card lands on an empty street lit orange.

---

1. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off
Still dark. Sodium light. Bins. A cashpoint glowing to itself. Traffic somewhere else. The street is empty.
Hold. Five seconds.

2. STATIC — 35mm, locked off
An OLD WOMAN (70s, good coat, handbag over her forearm) walks toward us at her own pace.

3. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, from the cashpoint end, looking back
A figure steps out of a doorway: the MUGGER (19, hood up, wired). He stands in her path.

4. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 50mm, locked off
A knife held low and tight against his own body. He is considerably more frightened than she is. She stops. Takes him in. Almost disappointed in him.
   MUGGER: Bag.

5. INSERT — 85mm, static
His hand. The knife, low and tight against his own body, shaking a little.

6. STATIC — 50mm, on the old woman
She's already unclipping the handbag, calmly, the way you'd hand over a bus pass.

7. STATIC — 35mm, two-shot
She holds it out. He reaches for it.
   MUGGER: Come on. Come on —

8. INSERT — 85mm, static
Both their hands on the bag.
   MUGGER: Just give it —

9. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, as 1
And he isn't there. No flash. No sound. The traffic in the next street carries on. The old woman stands alone in the frame, her arm out.

10. INSERT — 85mm, low, static
The knife drops. Hits the pavement. Rings. Lies still.

11. STATIC — 35mm, locked off
The Old Woman stands in an empty street with her arm out, offering her handbag to nobody.

12. STATIC — 50mm, on the old woman
She looks behind her. She looks up — briefly, instinctively — and is slightly embarrassed to have done it. There is no one to tell. She looks at the patch of pavement where he was. Concern. For him.

13. STATIC — 35mm, low
She clips her bag shut. Takes a tissue from her sleeve. Crouches carefully, one hand on her knee, picks up the knife in the tissue, and puts it in her handbag.

14. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off
She walks on at her own pace.

15. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, as 1
The street, empty, lit orange. Title card: LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE.
Hold.
