# NEW SCENE — THE COPS, SECOND BEAT — Episode 1

INT. POLICE CAR — NIGHT (PARKED). Their grammar exactly: static two-shot from the bonnet.
The same bottle of water in the cup holder, untouched. Short — ninety seconds. Its only job
is to end the episode's comic thread on a worse note than it started.

Cast: KATH. RAY.

1. STATIC TWO-SHOT — INT. POLICE CAR — 32mm, locked off, through the windscreen
Dark. Both of them lit by the dashboard. Kath eating. Ray not.
Hold. Four seconds.
   KATH: He'll be all right, that lad.
   RAY: Which lad?
   KATH: The one from the car park.
   RAY: He was thirty-odd.
   KATH: He looked younger.
   RAY: They do.
(Pause. Six seconds.)
   KATH: I only tasered him.
   RAY: I know.
   KATH: People get tasered.
   RAY: They do.

2. STATIC WIDE — EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET — 24mm, from across the road
The car, alone, parked across two bays in an empty street. Interior light on.

3. STATIC TWO-SHOT — INT. POLICE CAR — 32mm, locked off, as shot 1
   RAY: There's a form.
   KATH: Ray.
   RAY: I'm only saying there's a form.
   KATH: (a beat) Have you got the form?
   RAY: (immediately) No.
(Pause.)
   KATH: Then there isn't a form.

4. INSERT — INT. POLICE CAR — 85mm, static
The bottle in the cup holder. Neither of them has drunk any of it.

5. STATIC TWO-SHOT — INT. POLICE CAR — 32mm, locked off, as shot 1
Hold. Five seconds.
   RAY: Kath.
   KATH: Mm.
   RAY: Who do you think's in charge now?
   KATH: (no hesitation whatsoever) Us.

6. STATIC WIDE — EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET — 24mm, locked off — same framing as shot 2
The car in an empty street, interior light on, two people sat in it.
Hold. CUT.
