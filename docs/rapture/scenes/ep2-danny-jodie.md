# NEW SCENE — DANNY AND JODIE — Episode 2, their first appearance

INT./EXT. A HOUSE — DUSK. Their grammar: handheld, tight, dark, red practical light.
Never a clean wide. Three weeks in. This scene establishes only two things: they do
this now, and she is better at it.

Cast: DANNY (40). JODIE (11).

1. HANDHELD — EXT. BACK GARDEN — 35mm, dusk
A fence. Danny goes over it badly, catching his coat. Jodie goes over it in one movement and waits.

2. HANDHELD — INT./EXT. BACK DOOR — 28mm, close
He's got a screwdriver in the frame. It isn't going well. She watches.
   JODIE: It's not that sort of lock.
   DANNY: It is.
   JODIE: It's the other sort.

3. INSERT — 85mm, handheld
The screwdriver slips. His knuckle goes into the frame.

4. HANDHELD — INT./EXT. BACK DOOR — 28mm
Jodie leans past him, does something to the handle, and the door opens.

5. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm
Danny holding a screwdriver, looking at an open door.
   DANNY: It was already —
   JODIE: Mm.

6. HANDHELD — INT. KITCHEN — 28mm, red torchlight
Inside. Close and shifting. A cupboard door, a corner of worktop, half a calendar. Never the room.
   DANNY: (low) Rules.
   JODIE: (recited, bored) Nothing off anyone still alive. Nothing off the dead that's got a name on it. No upstairs.
   DANNY: And?
   JODIE: And check the cistern.

7. INSERT — 100mm macro, handheld
Cupboards opening. Tins. A great deal of chutney. Nothing useful.

8. HANDHELD — INT. KITCHEN — 35mm
Danny finds a four-pint bottle of water at the back of a cupboard. Half full. He's thrilled and trying not to show it.

9. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
She's looking at the ceiling.
   JODIE: There's a coat on the bannister.
   DANNY: So?
   JODIE: It's a going-out coat.

10. HANDHELD — INT. KITCHEN — 28mm
He stops. Listens. The house is completely still.
   DANNY: They're gone.
   JODIE: You don't know that.
   DANNY: Everyone's gone.
   JODIE: Not everyone.

11. HANDHELD — INT. HALLWAY — 35mm, very dark
Jodie goes toward the downstairs loo. Her bike light swings across a wall of framed photographs. Twenty of them, the same two people.

12. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm
She stops. Not moved. Assessing.
   JODIE: (calling, not loud) Dad.
   DANNY: (o.s.) What.
   JODIE: How many people d'you reckon are still here?
   DANNY: (o.s.) Don't know.
   JODIE: One in ten? One in twenty?
   DANNY: (o.s.) Do the cistern, Jodie.

13. INSERT — 85mm, handheld
The cistern lid off. She dips a bottle and fills it, entirely unbothered.

14. HANDHELD — INT. KITCHEN — 28mm
A sound upstairs. One dull thump.

15. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
Rigid. Not brave.

16. HANDHELD — INT. HALLWAY, FOOT OF THE STAIRS — 28mm
The two of them, close, whispering.
   DANNY: Out. Now.
   JODIE: It'll be a cat.
   DANNY: Out.
   JODIE: It's always a cat.
   DANNY: (hissing) One time it wasn't a cat.

17. HANDHELD — INT. STAIRWELL — 35mm, red, nothing visible
Hold. Then another thump, and something falling over.

18. HANDHELD — INT. HALLWAY — 28mm, chaotic
They go, fast and badly, knocking into things. He drops the screwdriver. She doesn't drop the water.

19. HANDHELD — EXT. ALLEY — 35mm
Both against a wall, breathing hard. Bags between their feet.
   JODIE: It was a cat.
   DANNY: You don't know that.
   JODIE: (holding up the cistern bottle) We could've had the upstairs one. Header tank's better.
   DANNY: (a beat) What's a header tank?
   JODIE: (genuinely pleased) Exactly.

20. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
A man who has just been overtaken by his eleven-year-old and is deciding whether to acknowledge it.
   DANNY: Don't tell anyone about the door.

21. HANDHELD WIDE — EXT. ALLEY — 28mm
The two of them walking away, red bike light bobbing, bags in hand. A completely dark street.
Hold.
