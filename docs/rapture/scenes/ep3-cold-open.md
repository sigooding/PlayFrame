# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 3 — COLD OPEN

Scene: EXT./INT. A PARK AND A GARDEN CENTRE — DAY
Cast: HARIEL (40s, linen, immaculate), SOQED (30s, likewise, slightly behind him always)
Grammar: the opposite of Hell's. Not surveillance — immaculate. Locked off, perfectly composed, centred, slightly too symmetrical. Wide lenses, everything in focus, beautiful light. It looks like an advert. That's the joke: they've made themselves look tremendous and they are of no use whatsoever.

---

1. EXT. PARK — STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, dead centre, golden light
An empty municipal park. A bandstand. Beautiful.
Two men are standing in the middle of the grass, perfectly composed, facing the same direction. They have been there for some time.
   HARIEL: Right.
   SOQED: Right.

   (Pause. Eight seconds.)

   HARIEL: Where would you keep a computer?

2. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, symmetrical
The two of them, centred, the bandstand exactly behind them.
   SOQED: I don't know what one looks like.
   HARIEL: You've seen one.
   SOQED: Have I?
   HARIEL: In the report.
   SOQED: I didn't read the report.

   (Pause.)

   HARIEL: (entirely unbothered) No. Nor did I.

3. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, frontal, two-shot
Both facing camera. Nothing behind the eyes, but well-lit.
   HARIEL: It's beige.
   SOQED: Beige.
   HARIEL: That's the main thing about it.

4. EXT. HIGH STREET — STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, centred, lovely light
An empty high street. The two of them walking down the middle of it in perfect step. Broken glass, an overturned bin, a dog in a doorway. Neither of them looks at any of it.

5. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off
They stop outside a shop. Look in the window.

6. INSERT — 85mm, static
A window display of beige garden furniture.

7. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, frontal
   SOQED: (quietly, to himself) Beige.
   HARIEL: Not that sort of beige.

8. INT. GARDEN CENTRE — STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, deep perspective, aisles
Enormous, empty, strip-lit. Racks of compost. The two of them walk down the centre aisle, still in step.

9. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off
They stop. In the middle distance, standing between two pallets of bark chippings, facing nothing: a MAN (60s, cardigan, still).

10. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, frontal, on the two angels
   HARIEL: There's one.
   SOQED: Is he ours or theirs?
   HARIEL: He's nobody's.
   SOQED: (a beat) Can we use him?
   HARIEL: Everyone's using them. That's what they're for.

11. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off
The blank continues facing nothing. Neither angel approaches him.

12. INSERT — 85mm, static
The blank's eyes. Nothing wrong with them. Nothing behind them either.

13. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 50mm, frontal
   SOQED: So what's the plan?
   HARIEL: We locate the equipment, we recover the equipment, we go home.
   SOQED: And if someone's using it?
   HARIEL: Nobody's using it. They haven't got the permissions.
   SOQED: How do you know?
   HARIEL: (with total confidence) Because they haven't.

14. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, deep perspective, aisles
The two of them walking away down the centre aisle, in step, immaculate, past the motionless man, in entirely the wrong direction.

Hold.

15. BLACK. TITLE CARD.
