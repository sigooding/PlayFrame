# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE FOUR — SCENE 4

Scene: EXT./INT. A HOUSING ESTATE — DUSK
Cast: NINA (45), ALAN (50s)
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, symmetrical. The camera never follows her. Long lenses for the road. Dusk rather than daylight for the first time, which should feel wrong in her thread — her light is supposed to be flat and bright. Only the vision breaks the grammar: handheld, broken, wrong aspect ratio, dropped frames, blown out, a hiss.

She is looking for a child's bedroom. She has no idea whose, or why.

The point: the pendant sends her to a bedroom and gives her no number, no door and no name, so she does the only thing available and audits thirty identical houses the way she would check a building for a gas leak. The one thing that lands is a table laid for four with nobody at it, and she does not stop for it. The right poster is in the wrong house and the wrong boy is in the frame; the vision matches and doesn't, and she has no idea what a Raptor is. Nothing answers twice. Alan's useless advice is the only reply she gets, and it is unarguable.

1. STATIC WIDE — EXT. DUAL CARRIAGEWAY — 135mm, locked off
The minibus, tiny, on an empty road, headlights on. Dusk, not daylight. It takes a long time to cross frame.

2. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. BUS — 40mm, locked off, from the passenger side
Nina driving. Alan upright, bag on knees, facing forward. Dashboard light on both of them.
Hold. Six seconds.
   ALAN: Are we looking for somewhere?
   NINA: A bedroom.
   ALAN: Whose?
   NINA: I don't know.
   ALAN: (entirely satisfied) Righto.

3. STATIC WIDE — EXT. ESTATE — 28mm, locked off, symmetrical, dead centre
A cul-de-sac of identical semis. Not one light on in any of them. The bus pulls up and stops in the middle of the road.

4. STATIC WIDE — EXT. ESTATE — 24mm, locked off
Nina gets out. Looks at the houses. All of them are the same house.

5. CLOSE — EXT. ESTATE — 65mm, static
Her face, lit by the bus's headlights. She gets the pendant out. Fist round it.

THE VISION — handheld, broken, wrong aspect ratio, dropped frames, blown out, a hiss.

6. FLASH — 25mm, unsteady
Wallpaper. Not this wallpaper.

7. FLASH — 50mm, harsh
Part of a poster. Big pixellated letters: — PTOR —

8. FLASH — low, six inches off the floor
Trainers under a desk. Moving.

9. FLASH — a cardboard box, packing tape, something beige inside.

10. FLASH — a green cursor, blinking.

11. CLOSE — EXT. ESTATE — 65mm, static
Nina, eyes open, in the headlights.

12. STATIC WIDE — EXT. ESTATE — 28mm, locked off
She looks at thirty identical front doors.
   NINA: (upward, flatly) Which one.

13. STATIC WIDE — EXT. ESTATE — 24mm, locked off
Nothing answers. Somewhere a dog barks. Not hers.

14. STATIC WIDE — INT. FIRST HOUSE — 28mm, locked off, hall
Torchlight from behind camera. An ordinary hall. Shoes by the door.

15. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. FIRST HOUSE — 50mm, locked off, a bedroom doorway
A child's room. Wrong child. Football, about seven years old, dinosaur duvet.

16. CLOSE — INT. FIRST HOUSE — 65mm, static, on NINA
She looks at it. Not it.

17. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. SECOND HOUSE — 50mm, locked off, a bedroom doorway
A teenage girl's room. Not it.

18. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. THIRD HOUSE — 50mm, locked off, a bedroom doorway
A box room full of an exercise bike and boxes. Not it.

19. STATIC WIDE — INT. THIRD HOUSE — 28mm, locked off, a landing
Nina on a stranger's landing at dusk with a torch, working systematically, the way she'd check a building for a gas leak.

20. STATIC WIDE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 24mm, locked off, the hall
She comes in. Stops.

21. INSERT — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 85mm, static
A dining table laid for four. Plates full. Nobody has touched them.

22. CLOSE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 65mm, static, on NINA
The only thing in the whole episode that gets through to her, and it's not the rapture — it's a laid table.
She looks at it for a moment. She puts her torch back up and carries on.

23. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 50mm, locked off, a bedroom doorway
A boy's room. Fourteen, maybe. Monitor, dark. Unmade bed. Poster on the wall.

24. INSERT — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 85mm, static
The poster. THE RAPTORS. Last season's. Correctly spelled.

25. CLOSE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 65mm, static, on NINA
She looks at the poster. She looks at the letters. It matches the vision and it doesn't. She has no idea what a Raptor is.

26. INSERT — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 85mm, static
The desk. Dust. No clean rectangle. Nothing has been moved off it.

27. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 50mm, locked off
She stands in a boy's bedroom holding a pendant, and nothing happens.
   NINA: (quietly, to the pendant) Is it this one?

28. STATIC WIDE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 28mm, locked off
Nothing. The room is just a room.

29. INSERT — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 85mm, static
On a shelf: a framed school photograph. A boy, twelve or so, in a blazer, mid-blink.

30. CLOSE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 65mm, static, on NINA
Wrong boy. Wrong house. Wrong poster. She's in a dead stranger's bedroom in the dark for no reason.
   NINA: (to the ceiling, sharply) There's thirty of them on this road.

31. STATIC WIDE — INT. FOURTH HOUSE — 24mm, locked off
Nothing answers.

32. STATIC WIDE — EXT. CUL-DE-SAC — 28mm, locked off, headlights
She comes out. Alan is standing in the middle of the road facing the wrong way. The dog is barking in the bus.
   ALAN: Any luck?
   NINA: No.
   ALAN: (cheerfully) These things take time.

33. CLOSE — EXT. CUL-DE-SAC — 65mm, static, on NINA
She looks at him. Genuinely considers leaving him on this estate.

34. STATIC MEDIUM — INT. BUS — 40mm, locked off
Both in. She doesn't start the engine. She sits with both hands on the wheel.
   NINA: Alan.
   ALAN: Yes.
   NINA: If somebody sent you somewhere and didn't tell you where, what would you do?
   ALAN: (no hesitation at all) I'd wait.
   NINA: For how long?
   ALAN: (warmly) Well. As long as it took.

35. CLOSE — INT. BUS — 65mm, static, on NINA
A woman who has just been given the worst advice available, by a man with no interior, and can't argue with any of it.

36. STATIC WIDE — EXT. ROAD — 135mm, locked off
The bus pulling away down a long dark road, headlights receding.
Hold.
