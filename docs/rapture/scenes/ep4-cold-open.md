# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 4 — COLD OPEN

Scene: INT. SUBURBAN FRONT ROOM — DAY
Cast: REEK (30s), TAMSIN (20s), GRAHAM (50s, a blank)
Grammar: fixed high-corner surveillance cameras, 4:3, faint burnt-in timecode, slight fisheye. Cut between fixed angles only. Never moves, never close. The timecode runs on across the cuts, so the audience can see how long this has been going.

Protections: the timecode does the heavy lifting — five hours pass and the joke is entirely in the numbers. "Wales" needs no reaction from anyone but Tamsin writing it down. Shot 15 is the first glitch the audience sees, played completely flat: no music sting, no camera move, just a man repeating a clause about payroll to an empty room.

---

1. FIXED CAM — high corner, 4:3, timecode 11:04:22
A front room. Graham in the armchair, hands on his knees, facing slightly away from anyone. Reek and Tamsin on the settee, sitting too upright, like people who have been told how chairs work.
   REEK: So. Where did they go.
   GRAHAM: Who's that?
   REEK: The bad ones.
   GRAHAM: (after a genuine pause) Bad ones.
   REEK: The wicked. The unrighteous.
   GRAHAM: (brightening) Oh, I don't know any of them.

2. FIXED CAM — opposite high corner, timecode 11:19:51
Same positions. Tamsin has her folder open on her knee.
   TAMSIN: Have you ever done anything wrong?
   GRAHAM: I took a pen from the office.
   TAMSIN: (writing it down) Right.
   GRAHAM: It was a rollerball.
   TAMSIN: When was this?
   GRAHAM: Ninety-eight.

3. FIXED CAM — high corner, timecode 11:41:07
Reek has got up and is standing over him. Tamsin is still writing.
   REEK: I want you to look at me.
   GRAHAM: Righto.
   REEK: (leaning in, doing something demonic and enormous with his face and voice) LOOK AT ME.
   GRAHAM: (looking at him) Yes.

   (Pause. Six seconds.)

   GRAHAM: Would you like a drink?

4. FIXED CAM — a different angle, the doorway, timecode 11:52:30
Graham has gone to make drinks. Reek and Tamsin stand in the middle of the room.
   TAMSIN: He's not frightened.
   REEK: They're all frightened. That's the whole thing about them.
   TAMSIN: He's offered us a drink.
   REEK: (a beat) He might be being clever.

5. FIXED CAM — high corner, timecode 12:08:14
All three seated again. Three mugs. Nobody has touched theirs.
   TAMSIN: Are you dead?
   GRAHAM: I don't think so.
   TAMSIN: Are you sure?
   GRAHAM: (considering it properly, for a long time) I'd have heard.

6. FIXED CAM — opposite corner, timecode 13:26:48
The light in the room has changed. Nobody has moved.
   REEK: Right. Once more. Four billion people went. Upstairs got the good ones. Where did ours go?
   GRAHAM: Ours?
   REEK: Hell's.
   GRAHAM: Oh, I couldn't say.
   REEK: Have a guess.
   GRAHAM: (a long pause, then, helpfully) Wales?

7. FIXED CAM — on Tamsin's folder
She writes WALES and underlines it.

8. FIXED CAM — high corner, timecode 14:02:33
Reek is sitting on the floor with his back against the wall. Tamsin is still going.
   TAMSIN: What do you do, Graham?
   GRAHAM: Payroll.
   TAMSIN: What's that?
   GRAHAM: (the most animated he has been) Well. It's the wages, but it's the deductions as well.
   TAMSIN: Deductions.
   GRAHAM: Statutory and voluntary.

9. FIXED CAM — opposite corner, timecode 14:47:19
Graham is still talking. Reek's head is tipped back against the wall. Tamsin's pen has stopped.
   GRAHAM: — and that's before you get to the salary sacrifice.

10. FIXED CAM — a wide fixed angle, timecode 15:31:52
Nobody has moved. Graham is still talking. The mugs have gone cold.
   GRAHAM: — which is where most firms come unstuck.

11. FIXED CAM — high corner, timecode 16:14:08
Reek gets up and walks out of frame. A door goes. Tamsin remains, alone with Graham.
   TAMSIN: (carefully) Graham. Have you ever made a decision?
   GRAHAM: (no pause at all) I can only apologise.

12. FIXED CAM — on TAMSIN
She looks at him. She writes it down. She has no idea what it means.

13. FIXED CAM — the doorway
Reek back in the doorway, coat on.
   REEK: Nothing in there.
   TAMSIN: He's given us Wales.
   REEK: He's not given us Wales.
   TAMSIN: (defensive) It's the only lead we've got.

14. FIXED CAM — high corner, wide
They leave. The front door goes. Graham, alone in his armchair, hands back on his knees.

He does not get up. He does not look round. He continues facing exactly where he was facing.

15. FIXED CAM — held. Timecode running.
Hold. Twelve seconds of a man sitting in a chair.

Then — no sound, no cut — he says the next clause of his sentence from shot 10, in exactly the same intonation.
   GRAHAM: — which is where most firms come unstuck.

16. FIXED CAM — held
Nothing. Timecode ticking.

17. BLACK. TITLE CARD.
