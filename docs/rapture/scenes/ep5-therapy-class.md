# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 5 — THERAPY CLASS

Scene: INT. THE SCOUT HUT — DAY
Cast: HI-VIS (clipboard, self-appointed facilitator), SUE (50s, blameless, furious), CARL (30s, knows exactly what he did), DEREK (60s, sober twenty-two years, insufferable), PAULINE (40s, wants to write to someone), DANNY (40), JODIE (11), MAN (40s, sweating)
Grammar: handheld, close, faces. A circle of chairs that isn't quite a circle. Red from the fire door light and a tarpaulin over a window. Never a clean establishing wide.

Protections: Shot 13's silence has to be long — the dead dog demolishes the room and they need a beat before they rebuild. Hi-vis's "what we're for" must be visibly invented on the spot, not delivered as doctrine. And Carl never says what he did, in this scene or any other.

This is episode five, with the last shot changed from "two days of water left" to the water being brown and rationed — which is where episode five sits anyway.

---

1. HANDHELD — 35mm, faces in the circle
Fourteen people. One empty chair, which nobody is addressing.
   HI-VIS: Right. So. This is a safe space.
   SUE: It's a scout hut.
   HI-VIS: It's a safe space, Sue.

2. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on HI-VIS
He has a laminated sheet he made himself.
   HI-VIS: And today I thought we'd go round and everyone says why they think they're still here.
   CARL: Do we have to?
   HI-VIS: It's voluntary.
   CARL: Right.
   HI-VIS: (a beat) But I'm writing down who doesn't.

3. HANDHELD — 50mm, on SUE
Arms folded. She's been waiting eight weeks for someone to ask.
   SUE: I'll go. I've never done anything.
   HI-VIS: Nothing?
   SUE: Nothing. Not a thing. I've never even had a parking ticket.
   PAULINE: You had that thing with the cat.
   SUE: (instantly) That was an accident and I put a note through the door.

4. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on SUE
   SUE: Forty years I've been careful. Forty years. And they took Linda.
   HI-VIS: Who's Linda?
   SUE: From work. Linda. She was horrible.

   (Pause.)

   SUE: So what was it all for?

5. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
Nobody's got an answer. Several people are nodding, because this is everybody's problem.

6. HANDHELD — 50mm, on PAULINE
   PAULINE: I think it's a mistake.
   HI-VIS: A mistake.
   PAULINE: A clerical thing. Like a mix-up.
   DEREK: It's not a clerical thing, Pauline.
   PAULINE: How do you know?
   DEREK: (with total authority) Because.

7. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on PAULINE
Entirely serious.
   PAULINE: So who would you write to?
   HI-VIS: (thrown) About what?
   PAULINE: About being left. Who do you write to.

   (A genuine pause. Nobody has considered this and now several of them want to know.)

   HI-VIS: I'll find out.
   PAULINE: Will you?
   HI-VIS: (writing it down) I'll look into it.

8. HANDHELD — 35mm, on CARL
He's been quiet. Everyone looks at him. He shifts.
   HI-VIS: Carl.
   CARL: I don't know.
   HI-VIS: You must have a theory.
   CARL: I haven't.
   SUE: Everyone's got a theory.
   CARL: (too quickly) I've not done anything either.

9. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on CARL
He absolutely has. He nearly says it. He doesn't.
   CARL: Nothing they'd take you for.

10. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
The room reads this as modesty. Two people nod at him supportively. Jodie, at the edge, does not.

11. HANDHELD — 50mm, on a MAN (40s, sweating)
   MAN: Can I say something? Because this is what I can't get past.
   HI-VIS: Go on.
   MAN: Rosemary next door. Seventy-eight. She had her dog put down.
   HI-VIS: Why?
   MAN: It went on her rug. New rug. Dog was eleven. She had it put down on the Thursday.
   HI-VIS: And?
   MAN: She went up. Straight up. I watched her go.

12. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on the MAN
   MAN: I've never done anything near that bad.

13. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle, a long hold
Silence. Because that's it, isn't it. That's the whole thing, and everyone in the room has just watched their own theory collapse.
   SUE: (quietly) So it's not about being good.
   PAULINE: Then what's it about?

14. HANDHELD — 35mm, on HI-VIS
And you can watch him get there. It takes about four seconds and it is entirely self-interested.
   HI-VIS: It's not what we've done.
   SUE: No?
   HI-VIS: It's what we're for.

15. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
Heads coming up. This is much better than the alternative.
   HI-VIS: They've not left us behind. They've kept us back.
   MAN: Kept us back for what?
   HI-VIS: (finding it as he says it) Work. There's work wants doing. Someone's got to keep order.

16. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on SUE
Forty blameless years, and she's just been promoted.
   SUE: (cautiously) That'd make sense of it.
   PAULINE: It would, actually.
   MAN: (relieved) We're here to do the Lord's work.
   HI-VIS: (writing it down) Maintain order.

17. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
Fourteen people reasoning backwards from a dead dog to a divine mandate, in about ninety seconds, with no objections.

18. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DEREK
Hand up. He does not need to put his hand up.
   DEREK: I've had a moment of clarity.
   SUE: (under her breath) Here we go.
   DEREK: And what I've realised is, I've done this before.
   HI-VIS: Done what?
   DEREK: All of it. Everything gone, nothing left, no idea how you get through the day.

   (Pause.)

   DEREK: I did it in 2003.

19. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
Nobody says anything. Derek is enjoying himself enormously.
   CARL: It's the end of the world, Derek.
   DEREK: (sincere) And?
   CARL: That's not the same as a divorce.
   DEREK: (a beat) It's very similar, actually.

20. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DEREK
   DEREK: One day at a time. That's all any of it is. You don't think about tomorrow and you turn up.

   (A long pause. And the awful thing is that it is the single most useful thing anybody has said in this room in eight weeks.)

21. HANDHELD — 35mm, on HI-VIS
He writes it down. Underlines it. He is not pleased about it.
   HI-VIS: (tightly) That's good, that.
   DEREK: I know.

22. HANDHELD — 35mm, the empty chair
The camera finds it. Nobody has mentioned it.
   SUE: Where's Brian?
   HI-VIS: (not looking up) Brian's not coming.
   SUE: Why not?
   HI-VIS: (a beat) He went out Tuesday.

23. HANDHELD — 35mm, the circle
A pause. Nobody follows it up. Sue folds her arms.

24. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
Eleven years old, at the edge, having worked out in the last twenty minutes that every adult in this room is guessing.

25. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
Sleeve down over the bandage. Saying nothing about anything.

26. HANDHELD WIDE — 28mm, red light, the hut
Fourteen people in a circle that isn't a circle, newly certain they've been chosen to maintain order, in a building where the water is brown and rationed.
Hold. CUT.
