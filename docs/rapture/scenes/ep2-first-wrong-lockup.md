# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 2 — "THE FIRST WRONG LOCKUP"

Scene: EXT./INT. ROADS AND AN INDUSTRIAL ESTATE — DAY
Cast: NINA (45), ALAN (50s)
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows her. Long lenses for the road. Only the vision is handheld: broken, wrong aspect ratio, dropped frames, blown out, a hiss.

---

1. STATIC WIDE, 135mm, locked off — EXT. DUAL CARRIAGEWAY. Long lens, heat shimmer. The minibus, tiny, on an empty three-lane road. It takes a long time to reach the middle of frame.

2. STATIC MEDIUM, 40mm, locked off — INT. BUS. From the passenger side. Nina driving. The dog on the seat beside her, upright, facing forward like a passenger.
   NINA: Don't look at me.

3. STATIC WIDE, 50mm, locked off — EXT. ROAD. Roadside. She passes a church. A queue outside it, forty people deep. A trestle table. Someone with a clipboard organising badly. Two men arguing at the front.
   The bus goes by without slowing.

4. STATIC WIDE, 35mm, locked off — EXT. ROAD. A roadblock made of three wheelie bins and a garden bench. A teenage boy in a hi-vis vest stands beside it with a cricket bat, entirely alone, guarding a road that goes nowhere.
   The bus drives around it across the grass verge.

5. STATIC MEDIUM, 40mm, locked off — INT. BUS. Nina, unbothered. The dog watches the boy go past.

6. STATIC WIDE, 135mm, locked off — EXT. ROAD. Hand-painted sheets tied to a footbridge. WE ARE THE CHOSEN ONES. Below it, on a second sheet: ROOMS AVAILABLE.

7. STATIC MEDIUM, 40mm, locked off — INT. BUS. She pulls over. Kills the engine. Takes the pendant out of her collar and holds it in her fist like it owes her money.

8. CLOSE, 65mm, static — Her face. Braced. Not reverent. The expression of someone about to get a phone call they've been dreading.

9. FLASH, 25mm, handheld — An orange roller shutter.

10. FLASH, 50mm, handheld — Strip lights in a corridor. One flickering.

11. FLASH, 50mm, handheld — A painted bay number. Two digits. Held a beat too briefly to be certain of the second one.

12. FLASH, 24mm, handheld — Low, six inches off the floor. A carpet. A radiator. Wrong decade.

13. CLOSE, 65mm, static — Nina. Eyes open. Breathing hard. She looks profoundly unimpressed.
    NINA: (to the windscreen) Forty-one, or forty-seven?

14. STATIC MEDIUM, 40mm, locked off — Nothing happens. The dog looks at her.
    NINA: Forty-one or forty-seven?
    (Pause.)
    NINA: (starting the engine) Right.

15. STATIC WIDE, 28mm, locked off — EXT. INDUSTRIAL ESTATE. A low unit behind chain link. Not the one Martin visited. Similar enough. Barrier arm down. Weeds.

16. STATIC WIDE, 24mm, locked off — She gets out. Looks at the barrier. Gets a pair of bolt cutters out of the bus and deals with the chain on the gate in about nine seconds.

17. STATIC WIDE, 24mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR. Deep perspective. A corridor of orange roller doors receding to a vanishing point. Strip lights. One flickering.
    She walks away from camera down the middle of it.
    (Same composition as Martin's corridor. Wrong building.)

18. MEDIUM, 50mm, static — She stops at 41. Looks at it. Cuts the padlock. Three attempts — she's stronger than Martin but it's still a padlock.

19. STATIC WIDE, 28mm, locked off — Low. The shutter goes up on a unit containing approximately four hundred plastic garden chairs.

20. MEDIUM, 50mm, static — Nina looks at four hundred garden chairs.

21. STATIC WIDE, 24mm, locked off — Corridor. She walks to 47. Cuts that padlock too.

22. STATIC WIDE, 28mm, locked off — Low. Shutter up. Empty. Completely empty. A concrete floor and a dead wasp.

23. CLOSE, 65mm, static — Nina in the doorway of an empty unit.
    NINA: (upward, conversational) Was it one of the other forty, then?

24. STATIC WIDE, 24mm, locked off — Corridor. Same framing as 17. Her, small, at the far end, in front of a corridor of identical shutters that goes on for a very long way.
    Hold.

25. STATIC WIDE, 28mm, locked off — EXT. ESTATE. She comes out. Loads the bolt cutters. Stops.

26. STATIC MEDIUM, 50mm, locked off — A MAN (50s, fleece, sensible shoes, holding a carrier bag) is standing beside the minibus. Perfectly still. He has been there some time. The dog is looking at him through the window.
    MAN: Is this yours?
    NINA: It is.
    MAN: I thought it might be.
    (Pause. He doesn't move or explain himself.)
    NINA: Can I help you?
    MAN: I've been here since Tuesday.
    NINA: Doing what?
    MAN: (a genuine pause, thinking hard) Waiting.
    NINA: For what?
    MAN: (cheerfully) Well. Nothing's come.

27. CLOSE, 65mm, static — On NINA. She looks at him. Twenty years of assessing people at a front door.
    NINA: What's in the bag?
    MAN: Bag for life.
    NINA: What's *in* it?
    MAN: (looking) Oh. Another bag.

28. STATIC WIDE, 28mm, locked off — Nina considers leaving him there. Properly considers it.
    Then she opens the passenger door.

29. STATIC MEDIUM, 40mm, locked off — Interior. He gets in. Sits perfectly upright. Puts the bag on his knees. Faces forward, exactly like the dog.
    NINA: Name?
    MAN: Al
