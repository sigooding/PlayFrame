# NEW SCENE — THE KITCHEN — Episode 4, scene 6

INT. MARTIN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — DAY. His grammar: fluorescent, flat, off-centre, locked off.
Her grammar wants symmetry, so the frames should sit slightly wrong all the way through; only the
street at the end is allowed to square up. Two protections: shot 15 — she asks once and then waits,
which is her whole character, and the pause has to be long enough to be uncomfortable — and shot 19,
where "It's mine" is the most honest thing Martin says in the series.

Cast: NINA. MARTIN.

1. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, slightly off-centre
A kitchen. Two mugs. Nina's own water container on the table between them, which is now a courtesy.

2. INSERT — 100mm macro, static
The tap, running brown into the sink. Nobody turns it off.

3. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 40mm, locked off, across the table
   MARTIN: He's at his nan's.
   NINA: Is he?
   MARTIN: (a beat) No.

4. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on MARTIN
   MARTIN: Everyone went. Didn't they.
   NINA: Not everyone.
   MARTIN: Well. Enough.

5. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on NINA
She puts both hands round her mug. She's about to tell a man something and she's not going to dress it up.
   NINA: He's not dead.
   MARTIN: Right.
   NINA: He's in a queue.
(Pause. Six seconds.)
   MARTIN: A queue.
   NINA: Mm.
   MARTIN: For what?
   NINA: Sorting.

6. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on MARTIN
A man who has been alone for eight weeks being told this by a nun in his kitchen. And the appalling thing is he's already halfway to believing it.
   MARTIN: How long's the queue?
   NINA: It isn't moving.

7. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 40mm, locked off
   MARTIN: Is he all right?
   NINA: He'll be bored.
   MARTIN: (nodding slowly) He would be.

8. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on MARTIN
   MARTIN: Will he remember it? After.
   NINA: I don't know. Probably not.
   MARTIN: (a beat) Does that matter?
   NINA: (honestly) I don't know that either.

9. INSERT — 85mm, static
Her mug. She hasn't drunk any of it.

10. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 40mm, locked off
   MARTIN: And you can get him out.
   NINA: No.
   MARTIN: You just said —
   NINA: That thing upstairs can get him out.
(Pause.)
   MARTIN: The computer.
   NINA: Yes.
   MARTIN: (flatly) It's from a storage unit in Wakefield.
   NINA: I know.
   MARTIN: There's no badge on it.
   NINA: I know.

11. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on MARTIN
He looks at the ceiling. At his son's room above them.
   MARTIN: It doesn't do anything. I've had it on for weeks. It's just a cursor.
   NINA: So it's broken.
   MARTIN: (defensive, immediately) It's not broken.
   NINA: What is it then?
   MARTIN: (a long pause) It's broken.

12. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, off-centre
Neither of them says anything for a while. The tap runs.

13. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on NINA
   NINA: Do you know anybody who could work it?

14. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on MARTIN
And this is the worst moment of his day.
   MARTIN: No.

15. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on NINA
She looks at him. Twenty years of people lying to her in kitchens.
   NINA: Right.
(She doesn't push. She waits. Eight seconds.)

16. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on MARTIN
   MARTIN: My brother.
   NINA: Where's he?
   MARTIN: He's got an arcade. Machines, cabinets, all the old stuff. He keeps it going.
   NINA: Ring him.
   MARTIN: Phones are down.
   NINA: Then we'll drive.

17. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 40mm, locked off
Martin does not move.
   NINA: What.
   MARTIN: Nothing.
   NINA: You've gone a funny colour.
   MARTIN: We don't speak.
   NINA: Why not?
   MARTIN: (a beat) There was a lot came up. In 2011.
   NINA: A lot of what?
   MARTIN: A lot. At auction. A job lot.
   NINA: And?
   MARTIN: And I bid against him.
(Pause.)
   NINA: And you won.
   MARTIN: (quietly) I did, yeah.

18. CLOSE — 65mm, static, on NINA
A woman being told that the end of the world is going to be fixed or not fixed depending on a fifteen-year-old grievance about a box of old computers.
   NINA: Martin.
   MARTIN: Mm.
   NINA: Is that why you never showed him that?
   MARTIN: (too fast) No.

19. STATIC WIDE — 24mm, locked off, off-centre
Held. He doesn't look at her.
   MARTIN: (eventually) It's mine.

20. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on NINA
She stands. Picks up her water container off the table.
   NINA: Get a bag. It's a long way and the petrol's going off.
   MARTIN: I haven't said I'm coming.
   NINA: (already leaving) No.

21. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, the hall
He sits alone in his kitchen for a moment. Then he gets up.

22. EXT. CUL-DE-SAC — STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, symmetrical — her grammar
The bus. Nina loading. Martin comes out of the house with a holdall in one hand and, inexplicably, a guitar case in the other.
   NINA: No.
   MARTIN: It's coming.
   NINA: It isn't.
   MARTIN: (getting in with it) It's coming.

22a. EXT. CUL-DE-SAC — STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off
Nina comes out of the front door carrying the terminal wrapped in the duvet off Max's bed. Martin behind her with the monitor. They load it into the back of the bus, carefully, between the water containers. The pendant stays round her neck.

23. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, from the road
The bus pulls away. The front door of the house is still open. Nobody shuts it.
Hold.
