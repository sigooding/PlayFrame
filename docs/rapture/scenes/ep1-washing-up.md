## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 3 — WASHING UP (revised)

Scene: INT./EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
Cast: NINA (45)
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows Nina. Long lenses for the road. Only the vision is handheld. Dry and procedural; Nina is unimpressed throughout, never amazed or afraid.

Fix: pendant is her mother's and she's always had it, so she doesn't find it on anyone's bedside. Shot 18 (the pendant among a dead woman's things) is cut. Shots 23–24 replaced.

This keeps the vision beat exactly where it was: she walks the corridor, her hand goes to her chest, and the thing she's ignored her whole life does something for the first time.
