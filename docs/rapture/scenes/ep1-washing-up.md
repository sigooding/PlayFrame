## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 3 — WASHING UP (revised — FIX 4)

Scene: INT./EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
Cast: NINA (45)
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows Nina. Long lenses for the road. Only the vision is handheld. Dry and procedural; Nina is unimpressed throughout, never amazed or afraid.

Fix: pendant is her mother's and she's always had it, so she doesn't find it on anyone's bedside. Shot 18 (the pendant among a dead woman's things) is cut. Shots 23–24 replaced — 23 STATIC MEDIUM 50mm her own room, 24 CLOSE 65mm picks up practical decision. Vision beat unchanged: corridor, hand to chest, first time.

---

1. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. ST JUDE'S HALL — DAY
The hall after the rapture. Chairs stacked against the wall. A mop bucket in the middle of the floor. On a clipboard by the door, one ignored complaint about water pressure from earlier — still there. Hold.

2. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
Nina washing up. Methodical. The tap runs brown, then clear. She doesn't react. Stacks a plate. The radio is off.

3. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
She dries the plates with a tea towel that says ST JUDE'S. Folds it afterwards, square. Puts it back on its hook.

4. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
She collects mugs from the hall. Three of them, all with different slogans. She carries them two at a time, like someone who has done this every day for years.

5. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. UTILITY — DAY
She checks the water butt by the back door. Half full. She tops up two bottles from it, labels them with masking tape: KITCHEN.

6. INSERT — 85mm, static — INT. UTILITY
The masking tape roll. The marker pen. Her handwriting, practical, small.

7. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
She pushes a mop down the corridor. The corridor is long, symmetrical, daylight from high windows. She doesn't look into the rooms as she passes.

8. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
She stops outside Deborah's old room. Door ajar. She looks in for a second, then keeps going. No pendant here. No bedside rummage.

9. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. STORE CUPBOARD — DAY
She counts tins. Writes a number on the clipboard. Tins, not water. Water is separate.

10. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CHAPEL — DAY
The chapel, empty. Six chairs. A wooden cross on the wall, slightly crooked. She straightens it without thinking.

11. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. OFFICE — DAY
She opens a filing cabinet. Inside, a list of residents with dates. She crosses out two names with a biro. No ceremony.

12. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
Daylight, overcast. She carries a black bag to the bins. The street outside is empty. No cars. She doesn't look up.

13. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. LAUNDRY — DAY
She folds laundry. Someone else's. She folds it anyway. Puts it on a pile.

14. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. LAUNDRY — DAY
The pile. She looks at it for a second, then leaves it. Someone will want it later, or they won't.

15. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
She checks the fire exit. Locked. She checks the log book. Signs it.

16. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
She stands in the corridor, alone. Listens. Nothing. She goes to her own room.

17. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. NINA'S ROOM — DAY
Her own room at St Jude's. Narrow single bed, made. Wooden cross on the wall, slightly crooked. A bedside drawer. She opens it.

18. CUT — Shot 18 is cut. Previously: pendant among a dead woman's things (Deborah's bedside). Removed — pendant is her mother's, always had it. No pendant found on anyone's bedside. This slot holds the cut.

19. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. NINA'S ROOM — DAY
She rummages in the drawer for torch batteries. Elastic bands, receipts, a rosary with a broken link, a nail file. She moves them aside.

20. INSERT — 85mm, static — INT. NINA'S ROOM
The torch, open. Batteries dead. She tips them into the bin.

21. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. NINA'S ROOM
She looks for new batteries. The drawer is a mess. She sighs, not annoyed, just occupied.

22. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. NINA'S ROOM
She finds a pack of batteries at the back of the drawer. Holds them up to the light. Checks the date on them.

23. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, her own room at St Jude's
A narrow single bed, made. A wooden cross on the wall, slightly crooked. She opens a bedside drawer. Among the junk — elastic bands, a rosary with a broken link, a nail file — a pendant on a cheap chain. Dull. Unremarkable.

24. CLOSE — 65mm, static
She picks it up because it's in the way of the torch batteries. Looks at it for a second the way you'd look at something you've owned for forty years and never worn.
She puts it on anyway. No reverence. It's a practical decision — she's packing, and it's smaller round her neck than in a bag.

25. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
She walks the corridor, hand goes to her chest. First vision — recorded, not divine. Wrong aspect ratio, dropped frames, blown exposure and hiss. The thing she's ignored her whole life does something for the first time. Handheld only for the vision.

26. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
She drives. Pendant bearing. The minibus on the empty road. Vision is tracker's recorded view, not divine. She pulls over, holds the pendant like it owes her money. The vision beat lands exactly where it was.
