# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 4 — COLD OPEN

Scene: INT. PAT'S HOUSE — DUSK
Cast: PAT (70s), MALCOLM (70s, cardigan, slippers, perfectly ordinary), GRAHAM (o.s., chained behind the cellar door)
Grammar: surveillance grammar, because she's a demon: fixed high-corner cameras, 4:3, faint burnt-in timecode, slight fisheye. Cut between fixed angles only. Never moves, never close.

The point: the cellar is planted before anyone knocks, so Scene 2 plays with the audience knowing there's a man under the floor. The man under the floor is Graham, and the phrase is his: the apology he gave Hell's intake with no pause at all now arrives from behind a locked door, which is its worst use so far. Pat and Malcolm's argument about method is the entire demon plot in eight lines — she's right, he can't accept it, neither will learn anything. The water cupboard is no longer bait; it's just a demon who doesn't drink, sitting on the most valuable thing in the county for no reason. She's waiting for nothing, and the burglars pick her house by chance. The whole open happens while the light goes: dusk at the first timecode, dark by the last.

Then Scene 2 is the old lady sequence as written — Jodie on the settee with the squash and the sweet tin, Danny in the kitchen, the knife and fork, the fork through the forearm, the bottles by the gate — except they picked her house by chance, and the audience already knows what is under the floor.

---

1. FIXED CAM — high corner, 4:3, timecode 16:12:04
A pensioner's front room in the dark. Curtains shut. Every surface immaculate.
Pat standing in the middle of the room. Not sitting. Facing the fireplace. Perfectly still.

Hold. Ten seconds. Nothing.

2. FIXED CAM — opposite high corner, timecode 17:40:19
Curtains open now. The dusk is failing at the window. Same position. Same stance.

3. FIXED CAM — the sideboard, fixed, slightly fisheyed
A row of framed photographs. The same woman in all of them. Three different men.

4. FIXED CAM — high corner, kitchen
She takes down the sweet tin. Empty. She tips in a bag of boiled sweets. Then another. Then another. Only boiled sweets, nothing else.
She levels the top of them with her hand so they sit flat.

5. FIXED CAM — kitchen, opposite angle
She opens the next cupboard along. Slabs of bottled water, floor to shelf, dozens of them.
She looks at them for a moment. Closes the cupboard.

6. FIXED CAM — high corner, the hall
The door under the stairs opens. MALCOLM comes up out of it, closes it behind him, and turns a key.

7. FIXED CAM — the kitchen
He washes his hands at the sink. Thoroughly. Up to the elbow.
   PAT: Anything?
   MALCOLM: He's apologised again.
   PAT: That's all he does.
   MALCOLM: I know what he does, Patricia.

8. FIXED CAM — high corner, kitchen, wide
He dries his hands and hangs the tea towel back on its hook, square.
   PAT: You want to leave him alone for a bit.
   MALCOLM: He'll break.
   PAT: He won't break. There's nothing in him to break.
   MALCOLM: (a beat, quietly furious) Then why does he keep apologising?

9. FIXED CAM — the front room
Pat sits in the armchair. Squares the doilies on the arms. Then adds another doily to a table that already has one on it.

10. FIXED CAM — the hall
Malcolm at the cellar door with his hand on the key. He doesn't go back down. He doesn't walk away either.

11. FIXED CAM — fixed on the cellar door, low angle, fisheyed
Held. Ten seconds.

From behind it, muffled, entirely calm and pleasant, in the intonation of a man doing what he's told:
   GRAHAM: (o.s.) I can only apologise.

12. FIXED CAM — the hall
Malcolm's hand on the key. He takes it off the key.

13. FIXED CAM — the front room
Pat in the armchair, facing the window, hands on the arms of the chair. Perfectly still. The street outside is dark now.

14. FIXED CAM — held, timecode running to 19:51:32
Hold. Twelve seconds. Nothing in frame moves.

Then, without any change in her posture, she smiles at the window.

There is nobody at the window.

15. FIXED CAM — the window from inside, fixed
An empty front path. A gate. A street in the dark. Nobody comes.

16. BLACK. TITLE CARD.
