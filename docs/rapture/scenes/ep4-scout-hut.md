# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 4 — SCENE 3

Scene: INT. THE SCOUT HUT — EVENING
Cast: DANNY (40), JODIE (11), a hi-vis MAN with a clipboard, a WOMAN (40s) who once did a course
Grammar: handheld, tight, close, red practical sources — a camping lantern, the fire door light. Never a clean wide.

The scene after the violence. He's bleeding, he has to explain nothing to anyone, and the group's only interest is the water. Protections: "Window frame" said twice with no elaboration is the whole of him — he can't lie and he won't stop. And the nurse noticing four holes in a row and deciding not to ask is the world the series is in now: everyone's got something they got somewhere and nobody's enquiring.

---

1. HANDHELD — 35mm, the doorway
Danny and Jodie come in. Four full bottles between them. Jodie's carrying three of them.

2. HANDHELD — 28mm, close
The hi-vis MAN with the clipboard is on them immediately.
   MAN: How much?
   JODIE: Twelve litre.
   MAN: (writing, genuinely impressed) Twelve.
   DANNY: There's more coming.
   MAN: More?
   DANNY: Tomorrow.

3. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
She looks at her father. He said it before he'd decided it.

4. HANDHELD — 35mm
People come to the table. The bottles go. Somebody says well done. Nobody asks where from.

5. HANDHELD — 28mm, a corner of the hut
Danny sits on a camp bed and gets his coat off with difficulty. The sleeve is stuck to his arm.

6. INSERT — 85mm, handheld, red light
The bandage. Soaked through. Four puncture wounds in a neat row.

7. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
She's got the first aid kit. She's not squeamish. She's already working out that four holes in a row is going to need explaining.
   JODIE: That's a fork.
   DANNY: Window frame.
   JODIE: It's four holes in a line, Dad.
   DANNY: Window frame.

   (Pause.)

   JODIE: Right.

8. HANDHELD — 35mm
She cleans it with bottled water, which is now an extravagant thing to do, and both of them know it.

9. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on DANNY
He watches her do it properly, tightly, the way someone's shown her.
   DANNY: Who taught you that?
   JODIE: You did.
   DANNY: Did I?
   JODIE: No.

10. HANDHELD — 28mm
A WOMAN (40s) appears with a mug. She's the nearest thing this group has to a nurse, which is to say she once did a course.
   WOMAN: Let's have a look.
   DANNY: It's fine.
   WOMAN: Let's have a look anyway.

11. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on the WOMAN
She looks at four punctures in a row. She looks at Danny. She says nothing about it.
   WOMAN: That wants antibiotics.
   DANNY: Right.
   WOMAN: We haven't got any.
   DANNY: Right.
   WOMAN: (entirely practical) So keep it clean and if it goes red up the arm, tell someone.
   DANNY: And then what?
   WOMAN: (a beat) Tell someone.

12. HANDHELD — 35mm, wider
She goes. Danny sits with his arm out. The hut carries on around him — the kettle, the rota, an argument about the rota.

13. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, two-shot, very close, quiet
   JODIE: We're going back, aren't we.
   DANNY: No.
   JODIE: You said there's more coming tomorrow.
   DANNY: There is.
   JODIE: From where?

   (A long pause. He doesn't have a lie ready.)

   DANNY: She leaves them by the gate.

14. HANDHELD CLOSE — 50mm, on JODIE
Eleven years old, working out that her father has entered into an arrangement with the thing that stabbed him, and that it's the right decision.
   JODIE: I'll come.
   DANNY: You won't.
   JODIE: She likes me.
   DANNY: (sharper than he means) Jodie.
   JODIE: She does though.

15. HANDHELD — 35mm
He doesn't answer. He looks at the hut. Twenty-odd people drinking tea made from water he got by being stabbed.

16. INSERT — 85mm, handheld
The rota on the wall. Someone has written DANNY in the WATER column for tomorrow. Not in his handwriting.

17. HANDHELD WIDE — 28mm, red light, the hut
Him on the camp bed with his arm out. Her packing the first aid kit away neatly.
Hold. CUT.
