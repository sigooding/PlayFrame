# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 4 — "NUMBER FOURTEEN"

Scene: EXT./INT. NUMBER FOURTEEN — NIGHT
Cast: DANNY (40), JODIE (11), THE WOMAN (number 14, 50s)
Grammar: handheld, tight, dark, red practical sources only.

---

1. WS, 24mm, handheld — the old van, stopped outside number 14 on a wet street. Headlights on. The red letterbox light on the front door. A 2-second pause. The headlights cut out.

2. MS, 35mm, handheld — the cab. Danny, a laminated card in his hand.
   DANNY: Number 14. The taps run there.
   JODIE: The letterbox is on.
   DANNY: So is the dashboard.
   A 2-second pause.
   DANNY: Rule two. No lights on the street.

3. MS, 35mm, handheld — Danny on the step, red light off the letterbox on his face. He knocks three times.
   A 3-second pause.
   DANNY: (to the door) It's cold.
   A 2-second pause.
   DANNY: (to Jodie, over his shoulder) It's very cold.

4. MS, 35mm, handheld — the door opens. THE WOMAN (50s, rumpled housecoat over a floral blouse, a Tesco bag stuffed with crumpled papers) holding the door for someone.
   THE WOMAN: Oh, sorry, I'm mid-sentence.
   She steps back into the hall, lets the door swing, and reopens it.

5. MS, 35mm, handheld — the hall, over her shoulder. A table set for six, plates on it.
   THE WOMAN: Number 14.
   DANNY: That's what the card says.
   THE WOMAN: I know what the card says.

6. MS, 35mm, handheld — the kitchen, red light only: the letterbox light under the door, the red light of the kettle on. Six cups on the counter.
   THE WOMAN: You want water.
   DANNY: We're looking for water.
   THE WOMAN: The tap. (points) Not the upstairs.
   DANNY: Why not?
   THE WOMAN: Dead most days.
   A 2-second pause.
   THE WOMAN: Was fine on Tuesday. That was Thursday.

7. CU, 50mm, handheld — the tap. The water comes out brown. A 4-second pause of brown water. The water comes out clear.
   JODIE: (quietly) It's doing it again.
   DANNY: Rule nine. Write it down.
   JODIE: I am.

8. MS, 35mm, handheld — Jodie filling the bottle at the sink. The woman watching.
   THE WOMAN: Biscuit.
   A 2-second pause.
   DANNY: We haven't broken any rules.
   THE WOMAN: Rule one is biscuit.

9. CU, 50mm, handheld — Danny. A slow blink. He looks at his card.
   DANNY: I don't have a biscuit.
   A 2-second pause.
   JODIE: I have a biscuit.
   Jodie produces a biscuit from Danny's jacket.
   DANNY: That's from the tin.
   JODIE: The tin's in the van. That's from my pocket.
   Danny looks at the jacket. There is no pocket.
   A 2-second pause.
   Danny writes something on the card.

10. MS, 35mm, handheld — the sink. Jodie offers the biscuit. The woman takes it, eats it, and hands the bottle back. It's full and cold and has the air out of it.
    THE WOMAN: Don't come back on a Tuesday.
    DANNY: Why not?
    THE WOMAN: The pipes.
    A 2-second pause.
    JODIE: That's not a reason.
    A 2-second pause.
    THE WOMAN: It's a reason.

11. MS, 35mm, handheld — the cab. Danny driving. Jodie holding the bottle.
    JODIE: What was she saying?
    DANNY: I think it was about the council.
    JODIE: To who?
    A 2-second pause.
    DANNY: I don't know.

12. WS, 24mm, handheld — the street. The van pulls away, no lights. The red letterbox light on number 14, on.
    A 2-second pause.

13. MS, 35mm, handheld — number 14's front door, from the street. The door opens. THE WOMAN, mid-sentence.
    THE WOMAN: — and that's why I'm telling the council, the parking, the noise, the —
    A 2-second pause.
    The door closes.
    CUT TO BLACK.
