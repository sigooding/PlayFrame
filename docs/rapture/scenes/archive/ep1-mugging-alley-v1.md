## EPISODE ONE — THE MUGGING

Scene: EXT. ALLEY — NIGHT
Cast: THE OLD WOMAN (70s, coat, handbag), THE MUGGER (30s)
Grammar: the camera keeps operating. No flash, no sound, no score, no reaction cut. Sodium streetlight and shopfront spill. The violence is texture, not event.

The first thing the audience sees. No mechanism is revealed. The knife ends up in the old woman's handbag, where it stays until the support group. The comedy is that the mugging is treated with exactly the same flatness as everything else in the series — the camera doesn't notice it's the cold open.

---

1. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, from the far end of the alley
Sodium light. A shopfront with its shutter down, spill from a streetlamp at the far end. The alley is empty. Bin bags against a wall.
Hold. Five seconds.

2. STATIC — 35mm, locked off, looking down the alley
An OLD WOMAN walks into frame from the left. Coat, handbag over her arm, sensible shoes. She's in no hurry. She passes the bin bags.

3. STATIC — 35mm, same setup, held
She keeps walking. Halfway down the alley now. Nothing behind her.

4. STATIC — 28mm, from the shopfront end, looking back
The other end of the alley. A figure — THE MUGGER — steps in and stands there. Hooded. Not moving. He waits for her to get closer.

5. STATIC — 35mm, the same angle as 2 and 3
She sees him. Doesn't stop. Doesn't speed up. Keeps walking at the same pace.

6. STATIC TWO-SHOT — 50mm, locked off
They're close now. He puts himself in her path. She stops.
   MUGGER: Bag.

(Pause. Three seconds.)

She doesn't give him the bag.

7. INSERT — 85mm, static
His hand. A knife — folding, small, nothing special. He opens it with one hand. The sort of thing you'd use to open a parcel.

8. STATIC — 50mm, two-shot, as 6
He holds the knife between them, not raised, not dramatic. Pointed vaguely at her midsection.
   MUGGER: The bag.

9. STATIC — 35mm, from behind the mugger
She looks at the knife. Then at him. Then at the knife again.
   OLD WOMAN: That's not yours.
   MUGGER: What?
   OLD WOMAN: The knife. That's not yours either, is it.

(Pause. Two seconds.)

   MUGGER: Just give me the bag.

10. STATIC — 50mm, on the old woman
She unhooks the handbag from her arm, slowly, the way you'd take off a seatbelt.
   OLD WOMAN: There's nothing in it.
   MUGGER: (o.s.) Give it here.

11. STATIC — 35mm, two-shot
She holds the bag out. He reaches for it. She doesn't let go.
   OLD WOMAN: There's a tin of mints. And a tissue.
   MUGGER: Let go.

12. INSERT — 85mm, static
Both their hands on the bag. His grip and hers. She's holding the clasp end; he's got the strap.

13. STATIC — 35mm, the same angle as 11
He yanks. She doesn't yank back — she just doesn't let go. He yanks harder. The bag tips. Something falls out of the open top and lands on the wet ground between them.

14. INSERT — 85mm, low, static
On the ground: a tin of Murray Mints. A folded tissue. A small purse. And a folding knife — different from his, older, with a wooden handle — that slides out of the bag and stops against his shoe.

15. STATIC — 50mm, two-shot, low angle
Both of them looking at the knife on the ground. Neither picks it up.
   MUGGER: (a beat) You've got a knife.
   OLD WOMAN: That's not mine.

(Pause. Three seconds.)

   MUGGER: Whose is it?
   OLD WOMAN: I don't know. It's been in there.

16. STATIC — 35mm
He lets go of the bag. Bends down. Picks up his own knife from the wet ground, folds it, puts it in his pocket. Then picks up her knife too. Looks at it. Looks at her.
   MUGGER: You want this back?
   OLD WOMAN: No.

17. STATIC — 50mm
He puts her knife back in her handbag. Drops it in without looking, the way you'd post a letter. Then he pockets her purse instead.
   MUGGER: There's four pounds in this.
   OLD WOMAN: I know.

18. STATIC — 35mm
He walks away, down the alley, past the bin bags, purse in hand. She watches him go. Then she picks up the mints, puts them back in the bag, picks up the tissue, puts that back too. She hooks the bag over her arm.

19. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, the same framing as shot 1
The alley. Sodium light. She walks away from camera, in the direction the mugger went, at the same pace as before. The shopfront. The bin bags.
Hold.
