# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 4 — COLD OPEN

Scene: INT. PAT'S HOUSE — MORNING
Cast: PAT (70s disguise)
Grammar: surveillance grammar, because she's a demon: fixed high-corner cameras, 4:3, faint burnt-in timecode, slight fisheye. Cut between fixed angles only. Never moves, never close. No dialogue.

The point: the audience learns what she is before anyone in the episode does. Protections: there is no dialogue in the entire cold open, so it plays as pure watching — and the surveillance grammar tells the audience which side she's on without a word. Shot 10's smile is the only thing she does that isn't domestic; everything else is a woman who has studied old ladies rather than been one.

Then Scene 2 is the old lady sequence as written — Jodie on the settee with the squash and the sweet tin, Danny in the kitchen, the knife and fork, the fork through the forearm, the bottles by the gate. 32 shots, already done.

---

1. FIXED CAM — high corner, 4:3, timecode 06:12:04
A pensioner's front room in the dark. Curtains shut. Every surface immaculate.
Pat is standing in the middle of the room. Not sitting. Standing, facing the fireplace, perfectly still.

Hold. Ten seconds. She does not move at all.

2. FIXED CAM — opposite high corner, timecode 07:40:19
The curtains are open now. Same position. Same stance. Still facing the fireplace.

3. FIXED CAM — high corner, timecode 08:03:55
She turns and crosses to the sideboard. Picks up a framed wedding photograph. Looks at it.
Puts it down turned slightly differently.
Picks it up again. Puts it back the way it was.

4. FIXED CAM — the sideboard, fixed, slightly fisheyed
A row of framed photographs. The same woman in all of them. Three different men.

5. FIXED CAM — high corner, the kitchen
She takes down a sweet tin. Opens it. Empty.
She opens a cupboard and takes out a bag of boiled sweets. Tips the whole bag in.
Then another bag. And another. Only boiled sweets. Nothing else.
She levels the top of them with her hand, carefully, so they sit flat.

6. FIXED CAM — the kitchen, opposite angle
She opens the cupboard beside it. Inside: slabs of bottled water, floor to shelf. Dozens of them.
She looks at them for a moment. Closes the cupboard.

7. FIXED CAM — high corner, the front room
She sits down in the armchair. Arranges the doilies on the arms so they're square.
Then she adds another doily to a small table that already has one on it.

8. FIXED CAM — the hall, fixed, looking at the front door
She goes to the door. Opens it. Looks out at an empty street. Nothing there.
She closes it. Opens it again. Looks out.
Closes it.

9. FIXED CAM — high corner, the front room, timecode 09:51:32
Back in the armchair, facing the window, hands on the arms of the chair.
Perfectly still.

10. FIXED CAM — held, timecode running
Hold. Twelve seconds. Nothing moves in the frame at all.

Then, without any change in her posture, she smiles at the window.

There is nobody at the window.

11. FIXED CAM — the window, from inside, fixed
An empty front path. A gate. A street.

12. BLACK. TITLE CARD.
