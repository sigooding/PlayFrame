## EPISODE ONE — ST JUDE'S AND THE RAPTURE

Scene: EXT./INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE — MORNING
Cast: NINA (45), BRIAN (50s), TERRY (50s), COL (30s), DEBORAH (60s), MAUREEN (40s), a VOLUNTEER (20s, lanyard, keen), twenty residents, a dog.
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows Nina; she walks into frames and out of them. Only the vision breaks the grammar. The rapture happens in this scene: no flash, no sound, no score; the radio carries on and the dog stays under the table. Dry and procedural; Nina is unimpressed throughout, never amazed or afraid.

---

1. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — EXT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE — MORNING
Pebbledash. A wheelie bin on its side. A hand-painted sign: ST JUDE'S. Noise from inside.

2. INSERT — 85mm, static
Beneath the sign, smaller: NO VISITORS BEFORE 10. THIS MEANS YOU, TERRY.

3. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off — INT. HALL — CONTINUOUS
Bedlam. Brian (50s) and Terry (50s) block the corridor, mid-dispute. Sister Nina Doyle (45, sleeves up, a crate of tins on her hip) is trying to get past.
   BRIAN: It's got my name on it.
   TERRY: That's not your name, that's nail varnish.
   BRIAN: In my handwriting.
   TERRY: It's my nail varnish.
   BRIAN: Why've you got nail varnish, Terry?

4. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, as she walks into frame
Nina, not stopping.
   NINA: Brian. It's a charger. There's four in the lounge drawer.
   BRIAN: Not with the fast bit.
   NINA: Then get up earlier.

5. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off, the foot of the stairs
Col (30s, freshly shaved, pleased with himself) comes down the stairs with a mug.
   COL: Four weeks Thursday.
   NINA: I know.
   COL: So I was thinking. Given that. The room at the back —
   NINA: No.
   COL: You haven't heard what I —
   NINA: You want Deborah's room because it's got the aerial. No.

6. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on Col
She's gone. Col looks wounded, briefly, then files it for another attempt.

7. STATIC MEDIUM — 35mm, locked off — INT. OFFICE — CONTINUOUS
Paperwork in drifts. A fax machine that has no business existing. Nina on the landline, pleasant, fluent, lying.
   NINA: Fourteen. ... No, fourteen. ... Then your records are wrong, love. ... Mm. ... Send someone. Tuesday's no good.

8. INSERT — 85mm, static, on the ledger
She hangs up and writes 22 in a ledger without pausing.

9. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. HALL — CONTINUOUS
Crate back on her hip. A Volunteer (20s, lanyard, keen) appears beside her.
   VOLUNTEER: Let me take that.
   NINA: No.
   VOLUNTEER: It's heavy.
   NINA: I know.

10. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off, the hall
She's past him.
   VOLUNTEER: I'll do the upstairs bins!
   NINA: Did them.
He stands there with his hands in his pockets.

11. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. UTILITY ROOM — CONTINUOUS
She dumps the crate. Deborah (60s, cardigan, arms folded, born to complain) is waiting for her.
   DEBORAH: The pressure's gone again.
   NINA: I'll look at it.
   DEBORAH: It's him upstairs. Forty minutes.
   NINA: It's not him upstairs.
   DEBORAH: Forty. I timed it.
   NINA: Why.

12. INSERT — 85mm, static, the tap
Behind them the tap coughs air. Water. Nothing. Water.

13. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off — INT. DINING ROOM — CONTINUOUS
A long table. Twenty residents. Toast, tea, a dog under the table, a local radio station going in the kitchen next door. Genuine bedlam. Nina finally sits. Maureen (40s) has been waiting for this moment all morning.

14. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, two-shot across the table
   MAUREEN: Can I ask you something religious?
   NINA: You can ask.
   MAUREEN: If someone's took something off you. And you take it back. But you take a bit more, for the inconvenience. Is that stealing?
   NINA: Yes.

15. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, as before
   MAUREEN: Even the inconvenience bit?
   NINA: Especially the inconvenience bit.
Maureen, considering this.
   MAUREEN: What if I put it back.
   NINA: Put it back, Maureen.
   MAUREEN: I'm only asking hypothetical.
   NINA: Put it back.

16. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, down the table, Terry at the far end
Nina stands and reaches for a stack of plates. At the far end, Terry is mid-anecdote.
   TERRY: — so I said to him, that's not a dog, mate, that's a —

17. STATIC WIDE — 28mm, locked off, as 16
Nothing. The table is empty. Twenty chairs pushed back. Full plates. A knife spinning slowly on a plate rim. No flash. No sound. The radio carries on in the kitchen. The dog is still under the table.

18. INSERT — 85mm, static, the plate rim
A knife spinning slowly on a plate rim.

19. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on Nina
Nina stands with both hands on the plates, still angled toward where Terry was. She does not move.

20. INSERT — 85mm, static, a cup and saucer
A cup rocks on its saucer. Settles.

21. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off, on Nina
She looks at twenty untouched breakfasts. All that food.
   NINA: Oh, for —

22. STATIC MEDIUM — 35mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — CONTINUOUS
She crosses to the radio, mid-jingle, and turns it off. Silence.
