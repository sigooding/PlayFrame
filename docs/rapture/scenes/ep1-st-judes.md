# LET THE RAPTURES COMMENCE
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 2 — ST JUDE'S AND THE RAPTURE

Scene: INT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
Cast: NINA (45)
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows Nina; she walks into frames and out of them. Long lenses for the road. Only the vision breaks the grammar. Dry and procedural; Nina is unimpressed throughout, never amazed or afraid.

The rapture happens inside this scene. No flash, no sound, no score. People are there and then they aren't, and the camera keeps operating as though the subject has merely walked out of frame. Nina doesn't know what's happened. She translates it into terms she already uses: someone has left, the house is short-staffed, the clipboard needs updating.

---

1. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
A Victorian semi. Overcast daylight. A sign by the front door: ST JUDE'S — RESIDENTIAL SUPPORT. The garden is tidy. Two wheelie bins, labels facing forward.

2. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
The hall. Chairs stacked against the wall. A mop bucket by the stairs. On a clipboard by the door, a sheet: WATER PRESSURE — COMPLAINT LODGED. Nobody has actioned it.

3. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
Nina at the counter, making tea. Two mugs. She puts sugar in one, doesn't taste the other. She knows who takes what.

4. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. SITTING ROOM — DAY
A RESIDENT (60s, cardigan) in an armchair, watching a television that isn't on. Nina brings him the sugared tea. He takes it without looking up.
   NINA: All right, Malcolm.
He nods. She leaves. The camera stays on him for two seconds after she's gone.

5. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
The corridor. High windows, daylight. Nina walks through from the right, carrying the other mug. She doesn't look into the rooms as she passes.

6. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. SMALL BEDROOM — DAY
A WOMAN (50s, dressing gown) sitting on the edge of a made bed, facing the window. Nina puts the mug on the bedside table.
   NINA: Tea, Deborah.
   DEBORAH: (not turning) Is it raining?
   NINA: It's been raining since Thursday.
   DEBORAH: I didn't notice.
   NINA: I know.

7. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
Nina back in the kitchen. She opens the cupboard under the sink. Pipes. She feels along one, runs her finger along a joint, looks at it. Dry. She closes the cupboard.

8. INSERT — 85mm, static — INT. KITCHEN
The tap. She turns it. The water runs clear, then brown for a second, then clear again. She turns it off.

9. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
Nina at the clipboard. She writes something on the water-pressure sheet. Below it, in different handwriting: CHECKED — ALL FINE. She looks at that for a second, then puts the clipboard back.

10. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. SITTING ROOM — DAY
The same sitting room. The armchair where Malcolm was. The mug of tea on the table beside it. The armchair is empty.

Hold. Three seconds. Nobody remarks on this.

11. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. CORRIDOR — DAY
Nina carrying a laundry basket down the corridor. She passes Deborah's door. The mug from shot 6 is on the bedside table, untouched. The bed is empty.

12. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. LAUNDRY — DAY
Nina folding sheets. Methodical. She doesn't count them. She folds the same sheet twice — the second time because the first fold wasn't square.

13. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
She runs the tap again. Brown for longer this time. She lets it run, watches it, turns it off. Opens the cupboard, looks at the pipes again. Closes it.

14. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
The hall. Chairs stacked. The mop bucket. The clipboard. On the residents' board by the stairs, six names. She takes a biro from behind her ear, crosses out two of them. No ceremony.

15. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. SMALL BEDROOM — DAY
Deborah's room. The mug is still there. The dressing gown is on the bed. Nina picks up the mug, feels it — cold. She takes it.

16. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. CHAPEL — DAY
The chapel. Six chairs, a wooden cross on the wall, slightly crooked. All the chairs are empty. Nina straightens the cross without thinking.

17. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — EXT. ST JUDE'S — DAY
Outside. Overcast. The street is empty. No cars. A postman's trolley by the gate, one bundle of letters on the seat, nobody with it. Nina takes the letters inside.

18. STATIC MEDIUM — 50mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
She puts the letters on the hall table. Sorts them. Two for Deborah. One for Malcolm. One addressed to RESIDENT — no name. She puts the others in a slot rack on the wall.

19. STATIC WIDE — 35mm, locked off — INT. HALL — DAY
The hall. The mop bucket. The clipboard. The water-pressure complaint, still there. Nina picks up the mop and pushes it down the corridor, away from camera. The hall is empty. The clipboard swings on its hook.
Hold.
