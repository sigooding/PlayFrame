# THE COPS - NIGHT

INT. POLICE CAR (PARKED) - STREET - NIGHT

Dark. Both lit by the dashboard. Kath eating. Ray not. The bottle of water in the cup holder, untouched.

Four seconds.

KATH
He'll be all right, that lad.

RAY
Which lad?

KATH
The one from the car park.

RAY
He was thirty-odd.

KATH
He looked younger.

RAY
They do.

A six-second pause.

KATH
I only tasered him.

RAY
I know.

KATH
People get tasered.

RAY
They do.

EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS

The car, alone, parked across two bays in an empty street. Interior light on.

INT. POLICE CAR (PARKED) - CONTINUOUS

RAY
There's a form.

KATH
Ray.

RAY
I'm only saying there's a form.

KATH
(a beat)
Have you got the form?

RAY
(immediately)
No.

KATH
Then there isn't a form.

Five seconds. Neither of them has touched the water.

RAY
Kath.

KATH
Mm.

RAY
Who do you think's in charge now?

KATH
(no hesitation whatsoever)
Us.

EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS

The car in an empty street, interior light on, two people sat in it.
