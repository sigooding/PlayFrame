# COLD OPEN

EXT. SIDE STREET - EARLY MORNING

Still dark. Sodium light. Bins. A cashpoint glowing to itself. Traffic somewhere else.

An OLD WOMAN (70s, good coat, handbag over her forearm) walks toward us at her own pace.

A figure steps out of a doorway. The MUGGER (19, hood up, wired). A knife held low and tight against his own body. He is considerably more frightened than she is.

She stops. Takes him in. Almost disappointed in him.

MUGGER
Bag.

She's already unclipping it, calmly, the way you'd hand over a bus pass.

MUGGER (CONT'D)
Come on. Come on —

She holds it out. He reaches for it.

MUGGER (CONT'D)
Just give it —

And he isn't there.

No flash. No sound. The traffic in the next street carries on.

The knife drops. Hits the pavement. Rings. Lies still.

The Old Woman stands in an empty street with her arm out, offering her handbag to nobody.

She looks behind her. She looks up — briefly, instinctively — and is slightly embarrassed to have done it. There is no one to tell.

She looks at the patch of pavement where he was. Concern. For him.

She clips her bag shut. Takes a tissue from her sleeve. Crouches carefully, one hand on her knee, picks up the knife in the tissue, and puts it in her handbag.

She walks on at her own pace.

The street, empty, lit orange.
