EPISODE ONE — DANNY AND JODIE

INT./EXT. A HOUSE — DUSK

Cast: DANNY (40), JODIE (11).
Grammar: handheld, tight, dark, red practical light — her bike light clipped to her coat. Never a clean wide, never the whole room.

# DANNY AND JODIE

EXT. BACK GARDEN - DUSK

A fence. DANNY (40) goes over it badly, catching his coat. JODIE (11) goes over it in one movement and waits for him.

EXT. BACK DOOR - CONTINUOUS

He's got a screwdriver in the frame. It isn't going well. She watches, a red bike light clipped to her coat.

JODIE
It's not that sort of lock.

DANNY
It is.

JODIE
It's the other sort.

The screwdriver slips. His knuckle goes into the frame. He swears without much conviction.

Jodie leans past him, does something to the handle, and the door opens.

Danny, holding a screwdriver, looking at an open door.

DANNY
It was already —

JODIE
Mm.

INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Dark. Red torchlight. A cupboard door, a corner of worktop, half a calendar. Never the whole room.

DANNY
(low)
Rules.

JODIE
(recited, bored)
Nothing off anyone who's still alive. Nothing off the dead that's got a name on it. No upstairs.

DANNY
And?

JODIE
And check the cistern.

Cupboards. Tins. A great deal of chutney. Nothing useful.

Danny finds a four-pint bottle of water at the back of a cupboard. Half full. He's thrilled and trying not to show it.

Jodie isn't looking at the cupboards. She's looking at the ceiling.

JODIE (CONT'D)
There's a coat on the bannister.

DANNY
So?

JODIE
It's a going-out coat.

He stops. Listens. The house is completely still.

DANNY
They're gone.

JODIE
You don't know that.

DANNY
Everyone's gone.

JODIE
Not everyone.

INT. HALL - CONTINUOUS

Jodie heads for the downstairs loo. Her bike light swings across a wall of framed photographs. Twenty of them. The same two people.

JODIE
(calling, not loud)
Dad.

DANNY (O.S.)
What.

JODIE
How many people d'you reckon are still here?

DANNY (O.S.)
Don't know.

JODIE
One in ten? One in twenty?

DANNY (O.S.)
Do the cistern, Jodie.

INT. DOWNSTAIRS LOO - CONTINUOUS

The cistern lid off. She dips a bottle in and fills it, entirely unbothered.

INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

A sound upstairs. One dull thump.

Danny goes rigid. Not brave.

INT. HALL - FOOT OF THE STAIRS - CONTINUOUS

The two of them, close, whispering.

DANNY
Out. Now.

JODIE
It'll be a cat.

DANNY
Out.

JODIE
It's always a cat.

DANNY
(hissing)
One time it wasn't a cat.

Up the dark stairwell: nothing. Then another thump, and something falling over.

They go. Fast, badly, knocking into things. He drops the screwdriver. She does not drop the water.

EXT. ALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Both against a wall, breathing hard. Bags between their feet.

JODIE
It was a cat.

DANNY
You don't know that.

JODIE
(holding up the cistern bottle)
We could've had the upstairs one. Header tank's better.

DANNY
(a beat)
What's a header tank?

JODIE
(genuinely pleased)
Exactly.

A man who has just been overtaken by his eleven-year-old, deciding whether to acknowledge it.

DANNY
Don't tell anyone about the door.

They walk away up the dark alley, her red bike light bobbing.
