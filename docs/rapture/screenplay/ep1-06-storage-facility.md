EPISODE ONE — MARTIN AT THE STORAGE FACILITY

INT. STORAGE FACILITY — DAY
Source: the episode-one screenplay draft of 21 September 2026 (docs/rapture/ep1-screenplay.md), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
No numbered shot board exists for this scene yet; this page is its only source.
Cast: MARTIN (40s); the ATTENDANT is unnamed and cast nowhere.
Grammar: flat institutional fluorescent, locked off, slightly off-centre. Aggrieved, never grieving. SUPER: THREE MONTHS EARLIER — a pre-rapture flashback, because the machine has to have sat in Max's room for eight weeks by the present.

# MARTIN

EXT. STORAGE FACILITY - DAY

SUPER: THREE MONTHS EARLIER

A low industrial unit behind chain link. A bored ATTENDANT in the booth, radio on, doesn't look up. The barrier lifts.

MARTIN (40s) drives through. Denim jacket that was correct in 1994. Hair long at the back on principle. Rings.

INT. STORAGE FACILITY - CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

A long corridor of orange roller doors receding to a vanishing point. Strip lights, one flickering. Martin walks down the middle of it with bolt cutters.

He stops at a bay and checks the number against a printed sheet.

INSERT - THE SHEET

An online auction listing. Lot number. A bad photograph. DECEASED ESTATE — ELECTRICALS, MISC. In the margin, in biro, underlined twice: GABE HOLLAND.

BACK TO SCENE

He cuts the padlock. Three attempts. He is not a strong man.

The shutter rolls up. Inside: one man's life in stacked boxes and bin bags. Guitar cases. A rolled rug. A wardrobe on its side.

Martin in the doorway. He doesn't move for a while. This is, transparently, the best day of his life.

He goes through it. A gold disc in bubble wrap. A box marked in biro: PERSONAL EFFECTS — DO NOT SELL. A hotel bill from 1980, itemised. He reads it like scripture. Two bottles of Scotch. A club sandwich. Room service, 4 a.m.

A tour jacket. He holds it up against himself. Shoulders. Length.

He puts it on. It fits.

He stands there in it under a flickering strip light.

Then he takes it off, folds it badly, and puts it back in the box.

A guitar case. He opens it and lifts the guitar out with both hands. He sits on an upturned crate and plays four bars of "DON'T CALL ME HOME". Badly. His fingers know the shape and not much else.

He stops. Puts it back. Closes the case.

His phone buzzes.

INSERT - PHONE

MAX: what time u back

BACK TO SCENE

He rings. It rings out. He looks at the screen.

MARTIN
(to nobody)
Course.

He goes back to the boxes.

From one of them: a beige plastic housing. Vents. A serial plate with no manufacturer on it. On the top of the casing, a small rectangular gap where something has been snapped off.

He turns it over. Looks for a make. A model. A badge. Nothing.

He runs his thumb over the gap. Shrugs. Drops it in with three other dead machines.

EXT. STORAGE FACILITY - CAR PARK - LATER

He loads the car. Several trips. He's careful with the guitar case. He throws the box with the machine in it on top of the others.

The car pulls out. The attendant lifts the barrier without looking up.
