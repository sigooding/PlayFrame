EPISODE ONE — ST JUDE'S AND THE RAPTURE

INT./EXT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE — MORNING
Source: the episode-one screenplay draft of 21 September 2026 (docs/rapture/ep1-screenplay.md), reproduced verbatim below its own heading.
The numbered shot board stays in docs/rapture/scenes/ep1-st-judes.md.
Cast: NINA (45), BRIAN (50s), TERRY (50s), COL (30s), DEBORAH (60s), MAUREEN (40s); the VOLUNTEER (20s) is unnamed and cast nowhere.
Grammar: locked off, wide, deep focus, daylight, symmetrical. The camera never follows Nina; she walks into frames and out of them. The rapture happens mid-anecdote with no flash, no sound, no score — the radio carries on and the dog stays under the table.

# ST JUDE'S

EXT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - MORNING

Pebbledash. A wheelie bin on its side. A hand-painted sign: ST JUDE'S. Beneath it, smaller: NO VISITORS BEFORE 10. THIS MEANS YOU, TERRY.

Noise from inside.

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Bedlam. BRIAN (50s) and TERRY (50s) block the corridor, mid-dispute. SISTER NINA DOYLE (45, sleeves up, a crate of tins on her hip) is trying to get past.

BRIAN
It's got my name on it.

TERRY
That's not your name, that's nail varnish.

BRIAN
In my handwriting.

TERRY
It's my nail varnish.

BRIAN
Why've you got nail varnish, Terry?

NINA
(not stopping)
Brian. It's a charger. There's four in the lounge drawer.

BRIAN
Not with the fast bit.

NINA
Then get up earlier.

COL (30s, freshly shaved, pleased with himself) comes down the stairs with a mug.

COL
Four weeks Thursday.

NINA
I know.

COL
So I was thinking. Given that. The room at the back —

NINA
No.

COL
You haven't heard what I —

NINA
You want Deborah's room because it's got the aerial. No.

She's gone. Col looks wounded, briefly, then files it for another attempt.

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

Paperwork in drifts. A fax machine that has no business existing. Nina on the landline, pleasant, fluent, lying.

NINA
(into phone)
Fourteen. ... No, fourteen. ... Then your records are wrong, love. ... Mm. ... Send someone. Tuesday's no good.

She hangs up and writes 22 in a ledger without pausing.

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Crate back on her hip. A VOLUNTEER (20s, lanyard, keen) appears beside her.

VOLUNTEER
Let me take that.

NINA
No.

VOLUNTEER
It's heavy.

NINA
I know.

She's past him.

VOLUNTEER
(calling after)
I'll do the upstairs bins!

NINA
(not turning)
Did them.

He stands there with his hands in his pockets.

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - UTILITY ROOM - CONTINUOUS

She dumps the crate. DEBORAH (60s, cardigan, arms folded, born to complain) is waiting for her.

DEBORAH
The pressure's gone again.

NINA
I'll look at it.

DEBORAH
It's him upstairs. Forty minutes.

NINA
It's not him upstairs.

DEBORAH
Forty. I timed it.

NINA
(flat)
Why.

Behind them the tap coughs air. Water. Nothing. Water.

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - DINING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

A long table. Twenty residents. Toast, tea, a dog under the table, a local radio station going in the kitchen next door. Genuine bedlam.

Nina finally sits. MAUREEN (40s) has been waiting for this moment all morning.

MAUREEN
Can I ask you something religious?

NINA
You can ask.

MAUREEN
If someone's took something off you. And you take it back. But you take a bit more, for the inconvenience. Is that stealing?

NINA
Yes.

MAUREEN
Even the inconvenience bit?

NINA
Especially the inconvenience bit.

MAUREEN
(considering this)
What if I put it back.

NINA
Put it back, Maureen.

MAUREEN
I'm only asking hypothetical.

NINA
Put it back.

Nina stands and reaches for a stack of plates. At the far end, Terry is mid-anecdote.

TERRY
— so I said to him, that's not a dog, mate, that's a —

Nothing.

The table is empty. Twenty chairs pushed back. Full plates. A knife spinning slowly on a plate rim.

No flash. No sound. The radio carries on in the kitchen. The dog is still under the table.

Nina stands with both hands on the plates, still angled toward where Terry was. She does not move.

A cup rocks on its saucer. Settles.

She looks at twenty untouched breakfasts. All that food.

NINA
(genuinely aggrieved)
Oh, for —

INT. ST JUDE'S HOUSE - KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

She crosses to the radio, mid-jingle, and turns it off.

Silence.
