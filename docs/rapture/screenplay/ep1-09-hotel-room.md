# TAG - 1980

INT. HOTEL ROOM - DAY

SUPER: 1980

An ordinary hotel room. Bed made. Nobody in it. Ashtray, clean. Room key on the dresser. A desk calendar. Dust turning in the light through the net curtains.

The air near the foot of the bed distorts. Barely.

A figure assembles out of light — not dramatically, like a photograph developing. He drops the last inch to the carpet and braces on his knees. First breath.

THE MAN (late 30s). He checks his own hands like they're borrowed.

In his palm: a small silver rectangle. Frost blooms across it and retreats. He turns it over, working out which way up it goes.

It unfolds. Quietly. Mechanical, not magical.

On the desk, components arrive and assemble into a beige terminal and a CRT. Cables find the wall socket on their own.

Clipped to the top of the casing: a small tracker. A single red light, slow pulse.

He notices it. Snaps it off. Looks at it for a beat — a small dull thing. He puts it in his jacket pocket without much thought.

He sits. Green phosphor. A cursor.

ON SCREEN:

USER PARAMETERS
APPEARANCE +20%
CONFIDENCE +20%
TALENT +30%
CHARISMA MAX
MUSICAL ABILITY 95
SOCIAL STATUS 50

The cursor passes over SOCIAL STATUS and leaves it alone.

He types with two fingers, hunting the keys. Then quicker. His face lit green from below. Concentration. No joy in it.

At the mirror, his features shift and settle better. He tilts his head to check.

A beard arrives. He runs a thumb along the jaw. Approves.

Across the room, a guitar materialises, leaning against the armchair. He watches it arrive and doesn't move for a second.

ON SCREEN:

SONG PARAMETERS
TEMPO 120
KEY G MAJOR
CHORD PROGRESSION IV-V-vi-IV
LYRICS _

He sits on the end of the bed and plays. Clumsy. Then competent. Then good. Fragments of "DON'T CALL ME HOME" come together. The light through the nets goes from afternoon to dark. The ashtray fills. He never looks up.

ON SCREEN:

IMAGE = ROCK STAR

At the mirror, a leather jacket resolves onto him. He looks at it. He doesn't smile.

MONTAGE — "DON'T CALL ME HOME", LIVE

— Newsprint. A record pressing.
— An arena from the back of the hall. Hands reaching. Flashbulbs.
— A backstage corridor: he fastens something round a YOUNG WOMAN's neck from behind. She's laughing at him. His hands and the chain, nothing clearer. Half a second.
— The crowd singing the chorus back at him.

END MONTAGE

INT. HOTEL ROOM - DAY - MONTHS LATER

A different hotel. The same geometry. Curtains shut mid-afternoon. A whisky bottle, glasses, room service going cold. Pills tipped onto glass.

He's slumped. Awake. Doing nothing.

His hand goes to his chest. He stands up wrong. He goes down.

The room: destroyed. He lies face down on the carpet. On the desk, the terminal glows.
