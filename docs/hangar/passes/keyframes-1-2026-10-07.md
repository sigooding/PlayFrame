## The rules the pass worked under

- **Scene 1 is black on purpose.** The screenplay holds black through the whole 1944 radio exchange,
  so shots 1–3 are *rendered* black frames at 1920×1080 — not generated pictures, not missing cards.
  They are what the film shows. The board says so in each card's `Picture:` line.
- **Every picture is 16:9 full-bleed, 1920×1080**, matching the house style block. The generator
  returned 1376×768; each frame was centre-fitted to 1920×1080, nothing letterboxed.
- **The prompts came from the board**, not from a neighbouring picture: each shot's own framing,
  lighting direction, mood, lens and cast, plus the `hangar` style prompt from `src/lib/styles.ts`
  (gouache Ghibli backgrounds, Iron Giant people, ambers/teals/blue-blacks, grain and halation).
- **Nothing is named or shown.** No prompt described or implied the thing in the crate; the word
  "alien", "robot" and "UFO" appear nowhere. Outdoors the light is flares, never flashlight beams.
- **Cast continuity sheet used in every prompt**: airman 22, short dark hair, neat civilian clothes;
  trucker 50, heavyset, cap, grey stubble, work jacket; sergeant 58, grey crew cut, fatigues.
- **Nothing was overwritten.** This is the folder's first content; from here the standing
  never-overwrite rule in `CLAUDE.md` applies to `public/images/hangar/` too.

## Honest caveats (retake candidates, not fixed in this pass)

- **Shot 4** reads smaller and more wooden than "enormous doors", and the generator lettered the
  building **HANGAR 4**; the screenplay's building is **Hangar 18**. Retake head of the queue.
- **Shot 5**'s television picture is legible as a committee table but the room reads a little tidier
  than a working guard booth.
- **Shot 6** carries the stencil, but check the wording reads `WRIGHT FIELD 1944 - INERT` at full
  size before this frame is approved.
- All thirteen are **Draft**, not approved; none has been through a director pass.

## What is left

37–39 (scene 8). Each card already names the exact file it is waiting for, and
`scripts/hangar/build-project.mjs` holds that list in `pending`; move a number from `pending` to
`keyframes` when its picture lands. `npm run verify:hangar` asserts the split, that every delivered
file exists on disk at 1920×1080, and that every undelivered card still says so.

**Image generation is capped at ten per turn**, which is why this pass stopped at 13.
