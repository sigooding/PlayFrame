## Honest caveats

- **17** puts street lamps and other traffic in the background; the scene is a dark two-lane back
  road with nothing else on it. Retake candidate. Her ID badge reads as a badge, as the board asks.
- **19** keeps both the cap and the mirror in frame, but they read as a tidy arrangement rather than
  mid-skitter.
- **21** holds its one rule: the trailer's interior is flat black and shows nothing. No crate is
  visible in the doorway, so the shot reads purely as the doors letting go.
- Still at the head of the queue from pass 1: **shot 4** letters the building HANGAR 4 (the
  screenplay's is Hangar 18) and its doors read too small.
