All 36 painted boards (4-39) were checked against their shot text, the prop and cast sheets, and each other. Shots 1-3 are black by design. Nothing was changed or retaken here; this is the work list for the next pass.

Rule for the pass: a retake goes in under the never-overwrite rule (archive the old file first, or use a new filename). Where a description is vague enough that a generator could make the same mistake again, fix the description first.

## Retake, lower priority: continuity

- **Trees and season.** 16 and 22 use giant redwood-like trunks and bare winter trees; 25, 31, 37 use spruce and pines; 23 has ferns. The film is the Ohio hills: leafy deciduous woods in summer fog. Fix by adding "Ohio hardwood forest, leaves on" to the shared scene text and retaking 16, 22, 25, 31.
- **Trucker's jacket.** Dark brown leather in 9, 12, 13 and 14 becomes **orange-red** in 27 and 29. Retake 27 and 29 with the same brown jacket.
- **Road line.** The centre line is yellow in 16 and white in 26. Retake 26 yellow.
- **17 Nurse.** The window is shut and reflective, but the text says the window is down. Retake with the glass gone and wind in her hair.
- **9 Hangar.** The airman is already in the cardigan, but the text calls him "in civilian clothes" only in the truck. Decide: keep him in uniform in the hangar and change in the cab, or accept civilian clothes from the start. This is a story call, not a picture fault.
- **7 / 6 Crate straps.** Teal straps in 6, grey in 7. Pick one (the prop sheet) and keep it.
- **25 Crate.** Two lid boards each show a ring; the crate has one lid and one ring.
- **35 Ring.** The coffee ring shows through on the inside face of the wood among the marks. It belongs on the outside of the lid only.
- **38 Airman's hair** goes ginger in the red light. Retake it brown.
- **11** The unmarked sedan "a quarter mile back" cannot be seen in the picture. Add "two small headlights far behind".
- **26** The wagon's taillights are one tiny orange dot. Make them two red points.
